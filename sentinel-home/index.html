Sentinel Home Edition

Private local AI for independence, communication, memory, and home control. Built from lived experience, not market research.

Rehability — First Tools Available → Our Vision Join the Interest List
First tools now available under Rehability: Marcus's Memory Helper — six local-first assistive tools for pain capture, appointment prep, medication tracking, letter drafting, daily memory, and call summaries. See Marcus's Memory Helper →
A note from Marcus O’Dell, Founder & Chief Technologist: I built Pocono AI from a bed, on a phone, while living with executive dysfunction, chronic pain, and the daily difficulty of turning urgent thoughts into clear action. Sentinel Home Edition is not a product born from market research. It is a product born from knowing, personally, what it costs when the tools that could help you do not exist yet — or are priced and designed as if you do not matter.
Why This Matters

People do not primarily need more apps.

For many people living with disability, chronic illness, neurodivergent cognition, aging, grief, fatigue, or caregiving burden, ordinary life tasks are not ordinary. Executive dysfunction, chronic pain, communication barriers, cognitive load, and illness do not respond to productivity frameworks. They require infrastructure.

Help me say what I mean

Communication barriers are not failures of intelligence. They are failures of infrastructure. Sentinel Home Edition drafts, rewrites, and translates what you mean into what you need to say.

Executive function support

When starting, sequencing, prioritizing, and finishing tasks are barriers — not character flaws — a system that scaffolds the day without judgment is not a luxury. It is infrastructure.

Home control without barriers

Turning a light on or adjusting a thermostat should not require a person in pain to stand, cross a room, or fight a confusing interface. Home control is dignity.

Privacy as a right

A person living with disability or illness should not have to hand their entire private home life to a cloud service to get the help they need. Privacy is not a premium feature. It is a right.

What Sentinel Home Edition Is

A private, local-first AI node for the home.

Sentinel Home Edition is planned as a local AI appliance — a dedicated, private computing node in your home that processes your data locally, stores your documents under your control, and assists you without routing your private life through someone else’s servers.

Local processing where it counts

The system is designed to run AI inference on local hardware. Your conversations, documents, reminders, and home routines are processed in your home — not uploaded to a cloud provider by default.

This is not a voice assistant that mails your words to a data center. It is infrastructure you own, in your home, under your control.

Connects to your life, your devices

Sentinel Home Edition is planned to connect with a phone, headset, tablet, or computer interface and integrate with approved smart-home devices — lights, fans, thermostat, television, doors, and more — through standard protocols.

You choose what connects. You choose what it remembers. You set the permissions.

Privacy-First by Design

Your home life is not a training dataset.

The same architecture that keeps medical records inside a clinic keeps your private life inside your home. Local processing, user-controlled permissions, encrypted storage, no default cloud upload of your home.

  • Local processing where feasible. AI inference runs on the local appliance. Private documents, conversations, and routines are not uploaded by default.
  • No default cloud upload of your private home life. The system is designed around the principle that what happens in your home stays in your home, unless you choose otherwise.
  • User-controlled permissions. You decide who can access the system, what they can see, and for how long. Permissions are revocable at any time.
  • Encrypted local storage. Documents and memory are stored with encryption on-device. Not in someone else’s database.
  • Audit trail for sensitive actions. Sensitive automations, access grants, and document retrievals are logged locally so you can see what happened.
  • Family and caregiver access only by your permission. A caregiver with access can only see what you have explicitly granted. Access is not inherited, assumed, or automatic.
  • Microphones and cameras are optional, disclosed, and not the default requirement. The preferred interface is headset or push-to-talk — not always-on listening.
Safety Boundaries

What Sentinel Home Edition will never do.

Clear boundaries are not limitations — they are the design. A system that claims to do everything, including things that require a licensed professional or a physical safety override, is a dangerous system.

  • No hidden recording. The system will not record audio or video without explicit, disclosed, ongoing consent.
  • No pretending to be a doctor, lawyer, therapist, or emergency service. Sentinel Home Edition drafts and assists. It does not diagnose, prescribe, represent, counsel, or dispatch. A human professional must always be consulted for clinical, legal, or emergency decisions.
  • No autonomous medical decision-making. The system does not adjust medication, contradict medical advice, or make clinical judgments.
  • No autonomous lock-in or lock-out without manual override and safety controls. Any door or access routine must include physical manual override capability. Human agency over physical space is always preserved.
  • No unsafe bath, water, stove, or electrical automation without hardware safeguards. Environmental controls that carry physical risk require proper hardware interlocks, not just software instructions.
  • No memorial or personality simulation without explicit, informed, ongoing consent. Any consent-based memory or archive feature will be clearly disclosed, easily revocable, and ethically framed.
  • Human override always preserved. Every automation, routine, or permission can be interrupted, revoked, or overridden manually. A person’s physical sovereignty over their own home is non-negotiable.
  • Dignity and agency always preserved. The system serves the person. It does not surveil, report on, restrict, or control a person on behalf of anyone else without that person’s explicit, ongoing consent.
Hardware Direction

Serious local hardware for serious local inference.

The premium Sentinel Home Edition is planned around a high-memory local AI workstation or appliance — hardware powerful enough to run capable AI models privately, without cloud inference, reliably enough to be trusted in a home environment.

Target architecture

Local AI appliance Phone / headset / tablet interface Home Assistant compatible Matter / Thread / Zigbee / Z-Wave integration Encrypted local storage Backup and recovery Permission system Audit log Optional professional installation

Hardware selection criteria

Hardware will be selected based on safety, reliability, local inference capability, serviceability, and cost — in that order. The current direction includes NVIDIA DGX Spark / Project DIGITS-class hardware or workstation-class alternatives, depending on final performance, pricing, and availability at the time of development.

No exact hardware is promised. The category and capability level are the commitment, not a specific product model.

Community Giveback Model

Paid installations help fund independence installations.

Pocono AI intends to use part of future profits to fund donated or heavily subsidized Sentinel Home installations for disabled people, chronically ill people, neurodivergent people, elders, and families in need who could materially benefit from the system but cannot afford it.

The principle

The long-term goal is that families who can afford private local AI help make it possible for people in need to receive it.

Private installations

Paid private installations at the planned price point support the engineering, deployment, support, and community program costs. Every private installation is a direct contribution to the giveback fund.

Community deployments

Once Pocono AI reaches profitability, the company intends to fund donated or heavily subsidized installations. The first deployments may be very limited — perhaps only one or two homes at first, depending on funding. The principle scales as the company scales.

Sponsors and partners

Organizations, disability advocates, foundations, and individuals who want to sponsor a future installation for a person in need are invited to express interest. No funds are collected at this stage — this is a planning commitment.

Development Status

Where we are, honestly.

First layer available — full platform still in development

What's available now: Rehability is the first implemented layer of Sentinel Home — six local-first assistive tools under Marcus's Memory Helper (pain capture, appointment prep, medication tracker, letter builder, daily log, call summary). These tools run locally, produce human-reviewed drafts, and require no cloud connection.

What is still planned: The full Sentinel Home Edition — the dedicated local AI appliance for the home covering communication, executive function, home control, safety, and independence — is a future plan. It is not currently available for purchase or installation. The statements below describe the intended direction.

Current focus

Pocono AI’s current work centers on the Sentinel Node infrastructure product for regulated professional environments — independent medical practices and small law firms. That product must succeed first.

Timeline

The current target is to begin planning, prototyping, or limited community deployment near the end of Year One or Year Two after Pocono AI reaches sufficient funding, pilot traction, revenue, and operational stability. No fixed date is promised.

What Sentinel Home Edition is

A community-facing extension of the same local-first, audit-first, human-override-always philosophy that guides the Sentinel Node. Same values. Different context. Built with and for the people who need it most.
