● Sovereign by Design

Your Data. Your Infrastructure.

Data sovereignty in AI is not a policy setting or a vendor promise. It's an architectural question: when your staff uses AI to process patient notes, case files, or privileged communications, where does that data actually go? With cloud AI, the answer is a vendor's data center. With Sentinel Node, the answer is: nowhere outside your network.

Language note: "Data sovereignty" and "sovereign by design" describe Sentinel Node's technical architecture — specifically, that AI inference runs locally and patient or matter data is not transmitted to external cloud infrastructure. These are design properties, not legal certifications. Covered entities and law firms remain responsible for their own compliance determinations. Human review remains the gatekeeper on all AI-assisted output.

What Data Sovereignty Actually Requires

True data sovereignty in AI requires more than a contractual promise from a vendor. It requires architectural properties that hold regardless of vendor policy changes, acquisitions, or service terms updates. Sentinel Node is designed around four structural pillars.

01

Local Inference

The language model runs on hardware inside your network. Queries are never transmitted to external cloud endpoints during inference.

02

Local Audit Trail

Every query and response is logged locally to a reviewable audit trail; SHA-256 hash-chaining is the next audit-layer release (Stage 2). Your audit records stay in your possession — not on a vendor's logging infrastructure.

03

Physical Custody

The Sentinel Node appliance is physically located at your practice or firm. You control physical access. The SSD data repository remains with you after service termination.

04

Signed Update Control

Software updates are Ed25519-signed and delivered on your schedule. No vendor can silently modify model behavior or data handling without your knowledge.

Why It Matters — By Practice Type

⚕ Independent Medical Practices

  • PHI in AI prompts creates uncontrolled data flows to vendors
  • Cloud AI logs may constitute disclosures under HIPAA
  • Local inference keeps patient data within the covered entity's perimeter
  • Audit trail supports accountability without cloud storage dependency
  • Air-gap option eliminates internet-based exposure pathways entirely
  • SSD retention after service end preserves your records

⚖ Small Law Firms

  • ABA Model Rule 1.6 requires reasonable measures to protect client confidences
  • Cloud AI routing of matter content may implicate confidentiality obligations
  • Local inference keeps privileged content on firm infrastructure
  • Matter-scoped isolation prevents cross-matter data bleeding
  • Local audit log supports work product documentation
  • No vendor receives case strategy or client communications

The BAA Misconception

A Business Associate Agreement creates contractual accountability. It tells you what a vendor is permitted to do with your data after they receive it. It does not prevent your data from crossing your network boundary.

Data sovereignty operates at the layer below the BAA. If AI inference runs locally, the data movement question never arises — there is no data to govern at the vendor level because no data was transmitted. The BAA still matters for Pocono AI's relationship with your practice, but the architectural question is answered by design, not contract.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does data sovereignty mean in the context of AI?

In the context of AI, data sovereignty means that the organization generating sensitive data maintains meaningful physical and operational control over where that data is processed and stored. With cloud AI, data leaves the organization's perimeter and is processed on vendor infrastructure. With a local inference architecture like Sentinel Node, data never leaves the organization's network — the AI comes to the data, not the other way around.

Why does data sovereignty matter for medical practices?

Independent medical practices generate protected health information (PHI) in ordinary operations. When AI tools process clinical notes, prior authorization requests, or patient communications, that PHI can be transmitted to cloud vendor infrastructure — often without staff awareness. Data sovereignty architecture keeps that processing local, so patient data remains under the practice's physical control.

Why does data sovereignty matter for law firms?

Attorneys have professional obligations to protect client confidences under ABA Model Rule 1.6 and equivalent state rules. When cloud AI tools process case strategy, deposition content, or privileged communications, that material crosses organizational boundaries. A data-sovereign AI architecture keeps matter content on the firm's own infrastructure, reducing uncontrolled data exposure through third-party vendors.

Does a BAA provide data sovereignty?

No. A BAA provides contractual accountability for how a vendor handles data after receiving it — it does not prevent data from leaving your network. Data sovereignty is an architectural property: it means data does not cross your perimeter in the first place. These are complementary, not equivalent. Sentinel Node's architecture addresses the data-movement question; the BAA addresses contractual accountability.

What does Pocono AI mean by "sovereign by design"?

Sovereign by design means data sovereignty is built into Sentinel Node's technical architecture — not achieved through policy or vendor promises. The inference model runs locally. The audit trail is local. The SSD data repository stays with the practice after service termination. These are structural properties that hold regardless of policy changes at Pocono AI.

What happens to my data if I stop using Sentinel Node?

The SSD data repository remains with your practice after the minimum service term. Pocono AI does not retain copies of your patient data or matter content in our infrastructure. Your data was never in our cloud to begin with — it stayed on your appliance throughout the service relationship.
