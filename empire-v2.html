Local AI Empire v2

How to Play

Read once, then click Begin Game. You'll figure out the rest.

🗺

Claim Territory

Click a tile to select it, then hit Claim. You can only expand to tiles adjacent to territory you already own. Cost: 2⚡.

⬡

Build AI Nodes

On owned tiles. Generates +1🖥 Compute and +1⚡ per turn. Mountain terrain gives an extra +1 Compute. Cost: 3⚡ + 2📡.

◉

Build Data Farms

Generates +2📡 Data per turn. Ruins tiles give +1 Data, Forest tiles give +1 Energy. Cost: 2⚡ + 1📡.

◈

Scout the Fog

Unexplored tiles are dark. Select any tile and Scout to reveal a 2-tile radius. Cost: 1⚡. Essential for reading enemy moves.

★

Win Conditions

Control 55% of the map — or capture the enemy ★ HQ for an instant win. You have 3 actions per turn. The AI has 3 too.

⌨

Controls

Click or tap tiles. Arrow keys move cursor. C claim  N node  F farm  S scout  ↵ end turn.
