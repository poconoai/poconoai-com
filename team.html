Team & Leadership

Building Pocono AI

Pocono AI is founder-led, building a focused team and advisory network around clinical workflow, patient advocacy, governance, strategic operations, infrastructure, and go-to-market execution. The roles below reflect how the company is staffed and supported today — including the work that is formalized, the work that is in progress, and active clinical research collaboration. We prefer accuracy over titles.

01

Founder / Leadership

Marcus O’Dell

Founder / Principal Architect

Founder of Pocono AI and architect of the Sentinel Node model: secure local inference, retrieval, auditability, and human-reviewed AI workflows for high-liability environments. Senior database administrator with 20+ years of experience hardening enterprise data systems. M.A. Psychology, Duquesne University.

02

Technology Leadership

Micah Yoder

Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Micah Yoder serves as Chief Technology Officer for Pocono AI, bringing a strong background in systems architecture and technical infrastructure. His role supports the design, hardening, and scaling of Pocono AI’s local-first AI systems, including Sentinel Node, Living Echo, and future governed AI appliance deployments.

Patient Advocacy / Clinical Outreach

Kaylynn Weikel

Kaylynn Weikel brings the patient voice into Pocono AI’s governance mission. 04

Business Leadership / Advisory

Travis Vought

Founding Strategic Advisor

U.S. Marine Corps Counterintelligence/HUMINT Veteran · Governance · Financial Crimes · Strategic Operations

Travis Vought is a U.S. Marine Corps Counterintelligence/HUMINT veteran and former financial crimes executive with over a decade of experience in governance, compliance, and strategic operations. As Founding Strategic Advisor, he helps Pocono AI shape product strategy, market positioning, partnerships, fundraising, and organizational development — translating complex technology into trusted, scalable business solutions.

His advisory work is core to Pocono AI’s mission: building accountable, local-first AI infrastructure that regulated organizations can understand, trust, deploy, and govern.

Advisory scope Travis advises Pocono AI on strategy, positioning, investor materials, partnerships, fundraising, and organizational development. His role is strategic and advisory; he is not part of the day-to-day executive team.

Brian Wirth

Fractional COO / Critical Advisory Board Member

Brian advises Pocono AI on business strategy, early-stage company development, leadership structure, go-to-market planning, and the transition from founder-led buildout toward pilots and formal partnerships. He works with the company in a fractional, advisory capacity.

05

Strategic Communications

Micky Dupree

Director of Strategic Communication and Advisor

Micky advises Pocono AI on strategic communication, professional messaging, public-facing clarity, and how the company explains complex AI governance and infrastructure concepts to real-world stakeholders — clinicians, attorneys, operators, and investors who need clear language without dilution of the underlying ideas.

06

Clinical Innovation & Systems Partner

Dr. Shreyash Kesarwani

Founding Clinical Innovation Partner & Clinical Systems Co-Architect

Perioperative Medicine · Anesthesia · AI-Integrated Clinical Systems

Dr. Shreyash Kesarwani is an anesthesiologist and researcher with interests in perioperative medicine, regional anesthesia, opioid-sparing analgesia, critical care innovation, and artificial intelligence in healthcare systems. His published work includes perioperative analgesia, regional anesthesia, pediatric anesthesia, and clinical workflow innovation.

Dr. Kesarwani contributes clinical perspective on physician workflow, perioperative systems, responsible AI design, and clinically realistic deployment pathways for Sentinel Node. His role supports clinical innovation strategy and systems architecture review.

“I believe in preserving quality of life and the intentional use of technology.”

Dr. Shreyash Kesarwani — Founding Clinical Innovation Partner & Clinical Systems Co-Architect

Outside medicine, Dr. Kesarwani enjoys playing drums, guitar, and video games.
