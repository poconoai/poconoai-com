How the Sentinel Node works

Your documents stay in a controlled environment inside your office. The AI reads only the sources you approve, produces a draft with citations, and a qualified person reviews it before anything is used. Every step is logged.

Start a pilot conversation See the demo

Ordinary cloud AI sends your patient or client documents to someone else's servers. Sentinel Node is built the opposite way: the work comes to the documents, and the documents stay with you.

The workflow, step by step

Understandable whether you run a clinic front desk or a law firm.

  1. 1

    Documents enter a controlled local environment

    Sensitive files are loaded into the Sentinel Node appliance that sits inside your own network. They are not routed to a public cloud AI service as part of normal operation.

  2. 2

    Approved sources are indexed

    Only the sources your practice approves — policies, guidelines, a matter file, a patient's records — are indexed so they can be searched. You decide what the system is allowed to see.

  3. 3

    The AI retrieves relevant passages

    When someone poses a question or task, the system finds the most relevant passages from your approved sources — rather than guessing from the open internet.

  4. 4

    A draft is produced with citations

    The AI assembles a draft, summary, or evidence packet and shows the citations behind it — so a reviewer can check each claim against the source it came from.

  5. 5

    A qualified human reviews — always

    A physician, attorney, or trained staff member approves, edits, rejects, or escalates the draft. Nothing is sent, filed, or acted on automatically. This step is not optional.

  6. 6

    An audit record is preserved

    What entered, what was retrieved, what the AI drafted, and what the reviewer decided are written to a reviewable audit log — so the work can be reviewed later.

What stays under your control

  • The documents, on hardware inside your network
  • The list of sources the AI is allowed to use
  • The final decision on every output
  • The audit log of what happened

What does not happen

  • No routine routing of your files to a public cloud AI
  • No autonomous filing, sending, or clinical/legal decisions
  • No output used before a qualified person signs off
  • No silent, unlogged activity
