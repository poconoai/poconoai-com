What the calculator assumes

  • 30% physician time reduction on EHR and admin tasks (conservative; pilot modeling basis)
  • 50% nurse time reduction on prior auth processing (conservative; pilot modeling basis)
  • 260 working days/year for physician cost basis
  • 52 weeks/year for nurse prior auth basis
  • Status quo cost includes your current cloud/scribe spend plus the dollar value of physician and nurse time currently lost to admin burden
  • Sentinel Node TCO is implementation fee + (monthly rate x 36 months). No hidden fees.

Estimate, not guarantee. Based on published research: 5.9 hrs/day EHR work [Sinsky et al., Annals of Family Medicine, 2016 (PMC5593724)]; 13+ nurse-hours/week on prior auth [AMA Prior Authorization Survey, 2024]; $82,975/yr physician admin cost [Casalino et al., Health Affairs, 2009]. Time-reduction assumptions (30% physician, 50% nurse) are conservative estimates from workflow modeling. Actual results vary by EHR system, specialty, and practice size.
