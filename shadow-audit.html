What shadow AI means for your practice
⚠Staff using ChatGPT, Claude, or consumer AI to handle patient or client documents creates an unlogged PHI transmission on every query
⚠HIPAA does not distinguish between intentional and accidental disclosure — shadow AI creates the same liability as a deliberate breach
⚠The breach notification clock starts at "knew or should have known" — not at the moment IT discovers the tool
⚠There is no audit record for shadow AI — if you cannot show what was transmitted, you cannot bound the exposure
