▌ THE GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLE

Governance bolted on outside the inference loop cannot constrain liability. The check has to happen where the data and the model meet — before the output is returned, before the human acts on it, before any system downstream has reason to retain it.

That is what makes a Sentinel Node different from a cloud assistant with a privacy policy: the governance and the model are the same artifact.
