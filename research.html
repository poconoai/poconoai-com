📋 Peer-Reviewed Research Studies

The administrative burden driving demand for the Sentinel Node is documented across leading medical journals, federal surveys, and health policy research. Every statistic Pocono AI publishes is sourced and linkable.

EHR Burden

Sinsky et al. — Annals of Family Medicine

Primary-care physicians spend 355 minutes (5.9 hours) of an 11.4-hour workday in the EHR — 269 minutes during clinic and 86 minutes after hours. The study established the foundational 5.9-hour figure that drives Sentinel Node design targets. Source: Sinsky et al., Annals of Family Medicine, 2016 (PMC5593724).

Read Pocono AI analysis →
Prior Authorization

AMA 2024 Prior Authorization Survey + MGMA 2024

Physicians spend an average of 14 hours per week on prior authorization — nearly two full clinical days. 94% report PA delays access to necessary care; physicians report that PA routinely leads patients to abandon recommended treatment. Per-physician annual processing cost: ~$68,000 (staff time, physician time, overhead, opportunity cost) — Source: MGMA 2024.

Read prior auth crisis analysis →
Legal Admin Burden

Legal Admin Overhead in Independent Firms

Attorneys in independent practices spend 30–40% of billable hours on non-billable administrative tasks including intake processing, document review, and correspondence drafting. The Sentinel Node targets 1.5–2.5 hours of daily reclamation.

Read legal burden analysis →
Patient Safety

Cognitive Load & Documentation Error

Documentation burden is directly correlated with physician burnout and cognitive load — a patient safety issue, not just an efficiency one. When physicians are rushed through documentation, error rates increase.

Patient safety research →
AI Liability

Federal Sanctions & AI Citation Fabrication

Federal courts have sanctioned attorneys for submitting AI-generated citations to cases that do not exist. Cloud AI models generate from parametric memory and will hallucinate with high confidence. Local RAG counters this by grounding every output in retrieved source documents.

Malpractice Trap Analysis →
Data Sovereignty

BAA vs. Physical Isolation

A Business Associate Agreement is a contractual promise made after your data has already left your building. Physical network isolation eliminates the transmission entirely — the compliance case is architectural, not contractual. HIPAA covered entities cannot waive this distinction.

📋 Sources Updated May 2026

All statistics on this page reflect the most current available peer-reviewed and government data as of May 2026. Key updates in this release:

  • HIPAA Breach Costs (2025): Healthcare data breaches averaged $7.42 million per incident in 2025 — the costliest of any industry for the 14th consecutive year. U.S. breaches averaged a record $10.22 million. Source: IBM 2025 Cost of a Data Breach Report via HIPAA Journal (July 2025)
  • OCR Enforcement (2025): 21 HIPAA financial penalties imposed in 2025, with the average settlement at $1.2 million. OCR expanded enforcement in 2026 to require proof of risk management action, not just risk analysis documentation. Source: HIPAA Journal, updated February 2026
  • Prior Auth — AMA 2024 Survey (hours & clinical harm): 14 hours/week per physician spent on PA; 94% of physicians report PA delays access to necessary care; physicians report PA routinely leads patients to abandon recommended treatment. Source: AMA 2024 Prior Authorization Survey
  • Prior Auth — MGMA 2024 (cost overhead): ~$68,000 per physician per year in fully-loaded prior-authorization processing cost (staff time, physician time, overhead, and opportunity cost of delayed care). Source: MGMA, 2024. Note: cost figure from MGMA — separate from the AMA burden/harm survey above.
  • Physician EHR Burden: Rotenstein et al. (Annals of Internal Medicine, 2024) measured 36.2 minutes of total EHR time per outpatient visit. After-hours ("pajama time") work has grown substantially since Sinsky 2016 (86 min/day); Rotenstein's data characterize the per-visit burden; daily totals depend on visit volume and are discussed with stated assumptions on the EHR burden detail page. Source: Rotenstein et al., Ann Intern Med, 2024 [DOI awaiting live verification — see ehr-burden-research.html]
  • AI Adoption in Small Practices (2025): Shadow AI — staff using personal AI accounts and consumer cloud tools to handle patient or client data — is an emerging governance and HIPAA risk, because interactions occur outside approved controls, BAAs, logging, retention policies, and audit trails. Regulators have explicitly referenced AI tool usage in recent enforcement actions. Supply chain attacks through business associates grew 42% year-over-year. Source: Healthcare Data Breach Statistics 2026 (March 2026) [industry blog — "fastest-growing vector" ranking language removed pending recognized-source confirmation]
