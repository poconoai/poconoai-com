Sentinel Node — AI on Your Hardware

Sentinel Node is a locally deployed AI inference appliance built specifically for independent medical practices and small law firms. It runs a capable open-weight language model on dedicated GPU hardware inside your network — not a cloud vendor's data center. Your staff uses AI. Your data doesn't move.

Status note: Pocono AI is in active pilot discussions. Sentinel Node is a pre-commercial product. Specifications, pricing, and capabilities described here reflect the current design and are subject to change. Pilot design partners are welcome — contact us to discuss fit. Human review remains the gatekeeper on all AI-assisted output.

What Sentinel Node Is

Sentinel Node ships as a managed service appliance. Hardware, model, software, governance layer, and support are all included in the monthly service fee. You do not provision cloud accounts, select GPU instances, or manage model installation. The appliance arrives configured, connects to your internal network, and your staff begins using it through a local interface.

The underlying AI model is an open-weight language model running on dedicated GPU hardware. All inference is on-premises. No query content — no patient notes, no case strategy, no privileged communications — traverses the internet during normal operation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sentinel Node?

Sentinel Node is a locally deployed AI inference appliance built by Pocono AI for independent medical practices and small law firms. It runs an open-weight language model on dedicated GPU hardware physically inside your network. All AI inference is on-premises — no query content is transmitted to external cloud endpoints. It ships as a managed service: hardware, model, software, and support are included in the monthly fee.

How is Sentinel Node different from a cloud AI subscription?

Cloud AI subscriptions route your queries to a vendor's infrastructure. Sentinel Node runs the model on hardware inside your network. Your patient notes, case content, and privileged communications never leave your perimeter during inference. Cloud AI is convenient and scalable; Sentinel Node trades scale for data sovereignty and local accountability — properties that matter in regulated environments.

What does the Sentinel Node service include?

The managed service includes: the inference appliance hardware, the open-weight language model, the Audit Proxy and governance layer software, onboarding and configuration support, SLA-backed uptime, Ed25519-signed software updates, and a Business Associate Agreement for healthcare deployments. Hardware is replaced or upgraded as part of the service relationship.

What does Sentinel Node cost?

Phase I starts at $950/month, Phase II at $1,495/month, and Phase III at $2,995/month. Pricing reflects hardware tier, model capability, and support scope. Contact Pocono AI to discuss which phase fits your workflow volume and requirements.

Is Sentinel Node available now?

Pocono AI is in active pilot discussions and seeking design partners. Sentinel Node is a pre-commercial product. If you are a medical practice or law firm interested in piloting local AI infrastructure, reach out through the contact page or download the Pilot Readiness Kit to assess fit.
