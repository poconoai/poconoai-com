What Pocono AI is

Pocono AI is an early-stage infrastructure company based in East Stroudsburg, Pennsylvania. We design, build, deploy, and govern on-premises AI systems for organizations where the consequences of a wrong answer are clinical, legal, or evidentiary—not cosmetic.

We do not sell access to a model hosted in someone else's data center. We deliver hardware, configure it under your governance, and remain the operating partner for as long as the engagement exists. The company is small by design. Engagements are small in number, large in depth.

02

What the Sentinel Node™ is

The Sentinel Node is a physical appliance. It is racked in your facility, sits behind your firewall, and runs the entirety of the AI inference workload locally. It has no outbound network requirement during operation.

Functionally, it is four things bound into a single sealed unit:

Local inference
Open-weight models running on the appliance's GPU. No external API calls. No vendor sub-processors.
Constrained retrieval
A citation-gate layer indexed only against documents your organization controls. Output may only assert what the retrieval layer can cite.
Append-only audit envelope
Every inference produces a reviewable audit record of prompt, retrieval, output, and reviewer identity. The full chain is reconstructable from one hash.
Human-in-the-loop
Regulated outputs require a licensed professional's signature before action. The reviewer's identity is bound into the envelope.
To be explicit: the Sentinel Node is not a chatbot, not a copilot, not an agent, and not a SaaS product. It is enterprise infrastructure delivered as hardware-as-a-service, and the governance properties above are architectural—enforced by the system itself, not by contractual promise.
03

The problem it solves

Regulated organizations face a structural mismatch. Cloud AI is operationally useful but creates uncontrolled exposure of protected health information, privileged work product, and proprietary documents. The exposure is not theoretical—it is a chain of sub-processors, retention policies, and training-eligibility ambiguities that the organization cannot independently audit.

The conventional responses are inadequate. Banning AI outright surrenders productivity to competitors. Accepting a vendor BAA and a click-through privacy policy is governance by signature, not by architecture. Building in-house is rarely feasible at the practice or firm scale.

Pocono AI exists for the gap in the middle: organizations that need the productivity, cannot accept the exposure, and require governance that is provable rather than promised. The structural answer is local inference under enforced human review with an append-only audit trail. That is what the Sentinel Node delivers.

04

Deployment model

The Sentinel Node is delivered as hardware-as-a-service. There is no software-only subscription tier. The hardware, the governance, and the operating relationship are the product.

Physical delivery
Appliance configured at our facility, shipped under tracked custody, racked in your environment, brought online behind your firewall.
Network posture
Zero outbound egress required for operation. The appliance does not phone home. Signed updates are delivered under your approval.
Operating relationship
Named contacts on both sides. Quarterly governance reviews. Direct line to the engineering team, not a ticketing portal.
Hardware refresh
Included in the engagement. You do not buy depreciating capital equipment; you retain a continuously maintained operational capability.
05

Governance model

Governance is enforced by the architecture, not by policy documents. Three mechanisms are structurally present in every inference:

Local inference
PHI and privileged material do not transit a public network. The appliance is sealed and air-gapped from external AI infrastructure.
Citation gate
The retrieval layer is the source of permitted assertions. Unsupported claims are flagged at the gate before a clinician or attorney sees them.
Audit envelope
Every output carries a reviewable audit record. Reviewer identity is bound in. The chain is reconstructable from one hash for evidentiary or regulatory review.

The governance posture satisfies HIPAA, attorney-client privilege, and state confidentiality obligations by architecture rather than by contractual promise. A full technical treatment is on the architecture page; the auditable detail is in the audit system documentation.

09

Expected outcomes

Outcomes depend on workflow scope and reviewer engagement. The following are typical for a well-scoped engagement at the end of the proof-of-value period:

  • Demonstrable air gap. Network logs confirm zero outbound AI-related egress.
  • Signed audit chain. Every regulated output reconstructable from its hash.
  • Workflow throughput improvement. Measured against the held-out task set defined at mapping.
  • Reduced legal exposure surface. No vendor sub-processor chain to audit.
  • Reviewer-bound accountability. Every regulated output carries a named signer.
  • Documented compliance posture. Architecture-level evidence for HIPAA, privilege, or board review.
