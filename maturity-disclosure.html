Maturity Disclosure

What we have, and what we do not yet claim.

Most AI companies describe a future and let you assume it already exists. This page does the opposite. It states plainly what is real today, what is still pre-production, and the claims we will not make until there is evidence to support them. We would rather lose a deal to honesty than win one on an overclaim.

As of June 25, 2026 · site release v400 · founder-led, pre-seed, pilot-seeking.

What exists now

Real today, in this repository or in working form.

  • A static public website and diligence library — the pages you are reading, with no trackers, no external fonts, and a published transparency audit.
  • A documented Sentinel Node architecture: local inference and retrieval, citation tracing, a reviewable audit log, and a hard human-review gate.
  • A browser demo that uses synthetic, pre-loaded responses to illustrate the retrieval-and-review workflow.
  • Local-first design principles — sensitive files are designed to stay inside the practice; no mandatory cloud AI API calls.
  • A pilot pricing scaffold ($5,000 implementation, $950/month per node) and a 60-day proof-of-value structure.
  • Governance and audit concepts: trust receipts, the near-miss doctrine, and fail-closed review patterns.
  • A founder-built technical foundation spanning Linux systems, vector search, and local AI infrastructure.
  • Physician and law-firm use-case mapping for the first bounded workflows we intend to pilot.
  • A plugin runtime (Sentinel Node Z1.97): plugin manifest validation, SQLite registry, plugin discovery, synchronous event bus, and worker supervisor.
  • Marcus’s Memory Helper — the first Rehability plugin: six working tool modules (Pain Episode Capture, Medical Appointment Prep, Doctor/Advocate Letter Builder, Medication/Treatment Trial Tracker, Daily Memory Log, Conversation/Call Summary) with local storage, non-medical boundary, and human_review_required on all exports.
  • A Rehability accessibility layer (Z1.89): big-button low-cognitive-load UI, six accessibility profiles, six quick-action cards, plain-language safety receipts, and print-first layout support.
  • Configured product-specific login surfaces (Z1.97): medical, law, engineering, home, Living Echo, and enterprise continuance surfaces — each login opens only the permitted product; demo user sees all paths.
  • Guided proof-of-value paths (Z1.92) for six product modes with synthetic golden-path demos and product-specific output previews.
  • Content backup and PITR planning, restore-drill evidence receipts, and network/firewall commissioning (internal; not a public-facing product yet).
  • EU/US AI transparency readiness synopsis that exports configuration and governance posture without exporting customer content (internal; not a public-facing product yet).

What is not yet claimed

Things we will not assert until evidence supports them.

  • Not a deployed enterprise clinical production system — we have not yet completed a measured production pilot.
  • Not a substitute for physician or attorney judgment. Every regulated output is a draft for a named human to review.
  • Not a HIPAA certification. Compliance is a property of an organisation and its workflows, not of a product.
  • Not SOC 2 complete. Security hardening and formal attestation are planned work, not finished work.
  • Not validated clinical-outcome software, and not cleared or evaluated by any regulator.
  • Not autonomous medical or legal advice — the system does not decide, it drafts and cites.
  • Not a guarantee that denials will be overturned, lawsuits prevented, or liability eliminated.
  • Not a claim of zero risk. We claim a smaller, locally controlled, auditable risk surface — not the absence of risk.

The claims boundary, in one paragraph.

Pocono AI does not replace clinicians, attorneys, compliance officers, or professional judgment. Sentinel Node produces cited drafts and evidence packets for human review. The public website and browser demos are educational, synthetic, and pre-production unless a page explicitly says otherwise. Where we describe the architecture, we describe what it is designed to do — not a certified, finished, or guaranteed outcome.

How we talk about compliance.

We use the phrase “designed for HIPAA-aligned workflows,” not “HIPAA compliant.” The difference is not legal hair-splitting — it is accurate. A product cannot be HIPAA compliant on its own; an organisation becomes compliant through its policies, training, agreements, and the way it operates its tools. Sentinel Node is built to support that posture by keeping sensitive files local, attaching citations, and recording a tamper-evident review trail. Whether your practice is compliant remains your determination, ideally with your own counsel and compliance staff.

The same discipline applies elsewhere: we say liability reduction and liability control, never “liability removal”; designed to reduce exposure, never “no risk”; pilot-candidate scaffold, never “proven in production.” If you ever find language on this site that overclaims, it is a defect — tell us and we will fix it.

Where we are as a company.

Pocono AI is a pre-seed, founder-led company building local AI infrastructure for small regulated practices. The primary risk is candid: we do not yet have a completed, measured production pilot. The first commercial proof point is not general AI adoption — it is measurable relief in one painful workflow where privacy, auditability, and human review matter. Funding and pilots unlock the first measured proofs of value, clinical and legal workflow validation, security and SOC hardening, an installer package, and a support model.
