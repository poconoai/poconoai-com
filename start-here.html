Sentinel Node Start Here
3-Minute Orientation

Start here. Pick your role. Three minutes per audience.

Sentinel Node is a governance-first local AI appliance for organisations whose work cannot leave the building. This page gives each audience — clinical, legal, IT and compliance, investors — the shortest honest path to understanding what you would actually be buying and how proof is established.

⚕Physicians & practices §Attorneys & firms ▣IT, compliance, operations $Investors & partners

First, the shared definition.

Sentinel Node is a physical AI inference appliance deployed inside the customer environment. The appliance, the retrieval index, the audit log, and the review interface all live behind the customer firewall. Nothing about a regulated query leaves the building.

The system drafts from documents your organisation explicitly approved, attaches citations to every claim, blocks unsupported claims at the citation gate, and requires a named licensed professional to sign off before any regulated output exits the workflow. The whole transaction — prompt, retrieval, draft, reviewer identity, decision — is sealed in a reviewable audit envelope.

It is not a chatbot subscription. It is not a cloud service with extra promises. It is governed local infrastructure delivered as hardware-as-a-service with a paid Proof-of-Value before expansion.

Route 01 · Clinical

If you run or advise a medical practice

≈ 3 min

You are carrying the documentation burden, the prior-authorisation crisis, and the malpractice liability that comes when AI is brought into clinical workflows without governance. The relevant question is not whether AI can draft — it is whether the draft is bounded, reviewable, and admissible.

Burden
Reduce administrative hours, not clinical judgment.
Prior auth drafting, policy retrieval, documentation assistance — bounded to the documents your practice approves.
Safety
No autonomous clinical decisions. Ever.
Regulated output remains draft until a licensed clinician reviews and signs. The system blocks claims it cannot cite.
Liability
An audit trail that holds up.
Every query has a sealed receipt — prompt, source, draft, reviewer, decision. Reconstructable from a single hash.

Route 02 · Legal

If you work with privileged legal material

≈ 3 min

Cloud AI sends work product through someone else’s infrastructure. That is a privilege problem and an ethics problem before it is a productivity problem. Sentinel Node keeps matter files, deposition prep, discovery review, and citation-bound drafting inside the firm perimeter.

Privilege
Work product never leaves the firm.
No external API calls. No third-party sub-processors touching matter files. Local retrieval against your own evidence index.
Evidence
Drafts that cite the underlying record.
Output is bound to the source material your firm approved — deposition transcripts, contracts, case files, internal memos.
Custody
Chain of custody for every AI step.
The audit log records who asked, what was retrieved, what was drafted, who reviewed, and what was decided.

Route 03 · IT & Compliance

If you carry IT, compliance, or operations

≈ 3 min

You need to know where the appliance sits, how updates work, what fails safely, what the support boundary looks like, and how the audit log proves what it claims. These are operational questions, not pitch questions.

Placement
Inside your perimeter, behind your firewall.
Rack-mount appliance on a segmented VLAN. No outbound dependency for inference. Air-gap supported.
Updates
Signed updates with customer custody.
Updates are signed, reviewable, and applied on a schedule you control. No silent push, no telemetry callbacks.
Failure
Fails safe, not invisible.
Retrieval failures and missing citations produce blocked drafts, not confident fabrications. Reviewer is always in the loop.
