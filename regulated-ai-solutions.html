Regulated AI Solutions, by Use Case

Pocono AI builds governance-native, local AI infrastructure for regulated work. These pages organize that work by who you are and what you are trying to do — for independent medical practices, small law firms, governance leaders, technical evaluators, and the people backing them. Every solution shares the same foundation: local inference, no cloud routing by default, human review, and a reviewable audit trail.

Language note: The pages below describe the technical architecture of Sentinel Node and how it is applied. Terms like “local,” “on-premises,” and “zero-telemetry” are design properties, not certifications. They are not HIPAA, legal, or compliance guarantees. Each practice or firm remains responsible for its own determinations, and human review remains the control point on all AI-assisted output.
