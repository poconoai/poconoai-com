What Sentinel Node is

Ordinary cloud AI sends your sensitive documents to someone else’s servers. Sentinel Node is built the opposite way — a managed appliance that sits inside your network, retrieves only from sources you approve, and produces drafts you can trace back to their source.

Built for physicians and attorneys who cannot gamble with cloud shortcuts. Governance-native AI for professional work: not a chatbot, not autonomous, not a black box.

For physicians & clinics

Prior authorization packets, denial responses, pre-visit chart summaries, and documentation support — drafted from the record and your guidelines, reviewed by the clinician. Less after-hours charting, with the physician as the decision-maker.

For attorneys & small firms

Discovery intake, deposition prep, matter chronologies, privilege-review support, and draft memos with citations — built from the matter file on your own hardware. Confidentiality preserved; attorney review mandatory.

How it works

Six steps — the same workflow whether it’s a patient record or a matter file.

1

Documents enter a controlled local environment.

2

Only the sources you approve are indexed.

3

The AI retrieves the most relevant passages.

4

A draft is produced with citations to those sources.

5

A qualified human approves, edits, rejects, or escalates — and it’s logged.

6

An audit record is preserved — every query, source, draft, and decision in the local log.

See the full workflow →

Why it’s safer than ordinary cloud AI

The design choices that matter when the documents are privileged or protected.

✓

Local-first. Documents stay on hardware inside your network.

✓

Human approval gate. Nothing is filed, sent, or acted on automatically.

✓

Source citations. Every claim traces back to the passage it came from.

✓

Append-only, reviewable audit trail. What entered, ran, and was decided is logged.

✓

Local by default. Nothing leaves the building without an explicit, logged decision.

✓

You set the boundary. You decide what the system is allowed to see.
