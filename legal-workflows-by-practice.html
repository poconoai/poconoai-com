For Attorneys · Practice-Specific Workflows

Where Sentinel Node fits — practice by practice.

A small general-practice firm doesn't have one workflow. It has four — or six, or eight — running in parallel, each with its own document patterns, its own pain points, and its own places where a missed detail becomes a real problem. This page covers the four practice areas a typical small firm runs, and how governed local AI fits into each.

Estate Planning Personal Injury Family Law Civil Litigation
01 · ESTATE

Estate Planning

Where one missed clause becomes a 20-year problem.

The pain

  • Template drift: clauses copied from one client's will end up in another's. Most firms catch it. Some don't.
  • Beneficiary cross-contamination across estate files — a name from the wrong family appears in a draft.
  • Amendment version control. A trust may have six revisions before signature. Which one is the operative one this morning?
  • Aging clients who change their minds. Capturing instructions accurately under time pressure, with a witness in the room.

The retrieval moment

Comparing draft language against the client's signed instructions, line by line, with every change annotated and unsupported additions flagged.

Sentinel Node · Estate Workflow · Local
"Summarize changes between trust_v4 and trust_v6 for the Hartman amendment. Flag anything in v6 that isn't supported by the signed client instructions."
matterHartman, R. — Trust Amendment (E-2024-188)
documentstrust_v4_draft.docx · trust_v6_draft.docx · client_instructions_signed.pdf
scopeBounded to E-2024-188 folder · No external retrieval
result7 supported changes · 2 unsupported changes flagged

Provision 4(b) in v6 has no supporting instruction. System tagged: UNSUPPORTED · attorney review required. Underlying paragraph displayed inline for the attorney to read.

The governance gate

⊘ SYSTEM REFUSES

Sentinel Node will not autonomously draft attestation clauses, notarization language, or signatory blocks. Witness and execution language must originate from attorney choice — not from a template suggestion. Any prompt requesting AI-drafted attestation returns a documented refusal citing the firm's estate practice policy.

The audit moment

▣ ENVELOPE CAPTURES

Which version of the client's instructions sourced which draft sections. Which paragraphs were AI-summarized vs. attorney-written. Which version of the trust the attorney signed off on for execution. If the will is ever contested, the firm can produce the exact provenance chain from first client meeting through final execution.

02 · PI

Personal Injury

Where intake speed and conflict gaps decide if you take the case.

The pain

  • Intake chaos under emotional pressure. A grieving client on the phone; the legal assistant trying to fill twelve fields on a worksheet.
  • Conflict checks across years of closed files. The conflict you missed is the one that ends your career.
  • Medical record retrieval from PDF stacks — emergency room, primary care, specialist, follow-up. Hundreds of pages.
  • Statute-of-limitations math, especially when the client doesn't know the exact accident date.

The retrieval moment

Running a deep conflict check across eleven years of closed and active matters before a new client is accepted.

Sentinel Node · Conflict Check · Bounded Local Retrieval
"Have we ever represented anyone connected to McAllister Delivery Services, Carmen Reyes, or Edward Reyes in any matter at this firm?"
searched11 years · 2,847 closed files · 140 active matters
match1 result — Carmen Reyes appeared as witness in PI-2019-31
matchNo results for McAllister Delivery or Edward Reyes
resolutionwitness ≠ client · flagged NO CONFLICT pending attorney review

Attorney clicks through to the 2019 file to confirm the witness role. Eleven seconds. The conflict check itself becomes part of the permanent audit record — when the bar disciplinary board asks "did you run a conflict check?" there is a sealed envelope with a timestamp.

The governance gate

⊘ SYSTEM REFUSES

Sentinel Node will not draft demand letters. Will not estimate case value. Will not assess liability percentages. Will not make settlement recommendations. Advocacy and valuation belong to the attorney. What the system will do: surface medical records, structure intake worksheets, run conflict checks, retrieve precedent settlement ranges from the firm's own historical files for attorney reference.

The audit moment

▣ ENVELOPE CAPTURES

Every fact assertion in a case file bound to its source document with Bates-stamped citation. When opposing counsel demands discovery, the firm can produce not just the documents but the provenance trail showing how each fact entered the file. Conflict-check envelopes are retained for the life of the matter plus seven years.

03 · FAMILY

Family Law

Where the file is decades-old and the hearing is tomorrow.

The pain

  • Prior orders scattered across years, judges, and counties — some scanned, some handwritten, some only in opposing counsel's possession.
  • Custody schedule reconstruction from a sequence of nine modifications, each amending the previous one.
  • Opposing counsel surprises at hearing: a provision from a 2022 order you didn't know was still cited.
  • Emotional family dynamics layered over operational complexity. The work doesn't stop being painful.

The retrieval moment

Surfacing every prior order in a custody matter — with operative schedules extracted and uncertainty flagged on handwritten exhibits.

Sentinel Node · Family Law · Citation-Bound Retrieval
"Surface every prior order in Donnelly v. Donnelly. For each: date entered, judge, operative custody schedule, and any provisions about religious upbringing or relocation."
matterDonnelly v. Donnelly (F-2021-44)
results9 prior orders returned · 2021 — 2025
parsed8 orders cleanly parsed · structured table generated
flagged1 order · 2022 interim · OCR LOW CONFIDENCE · handwritten by judge

For the 2022 handwritten interim order, the system displays the original scanned image inline rather than guessing at the contents. The AI does not pretend to know what the judge wrote. The attorney reads the handwriting directly.

The governance gate

⊘ SYSTEM REFUSES

Sentinel Node will not opine on the best interests of a child. Will not recommend custody outcomes. Will not draft argument language for hearings. Family law arguments are too consequential and too contextual for templated reasoning. What the system surfaces: prior orders, the operative provisions, the relevant case law from PA family courts the firm has uploaded, and the timeline of who agreed to what when.

The audit moment

▣ ENVELOPE CAPTURES

OCR confidence band on every scanned or handwritten document. Which order's provisions the attorney relied on in preparing for the hearing. If opposing counsel later argues the firm misinterpreted an order, the envelope shows exactly which version was reviewed, when, and whether the system flagged uncertainty.

04 · CIVIL

Civil Litigation

Where the Saturday spent reviewing discovery is the Saturday you wanted back.

The pain

  • Discovery review at scale — 1,400-page document productions where the relevant 23 pages decide the case.
  • Citation verification under deadline. The brief is due Friday. The associate has been working since 6 AM.
  • Cross-references between deposition transcripts, contract clauses, and email threads spanning months.
  • Hallucinated case law in AI-assisted drafts. A real and recurring nightmare in 2023-2026.

The retrieval moment

Finding every relevant document across a discovery production, with each excerpt bound to a Bates-stamped citation and any paraphrasing flagged.

Sentinel Node · Civil Litigation · Sealed Evidence Index
"For the breach claim under Count II, find every document referencing the modified delivery schedule between October 12 and November 30. Give me document name, date, speaker, relevant quoted language, and a citation back to the file."
matterHalverson v. Brookfield Industries (C-2025-9)
index size1,400 pages · sealed Bates-stamped production
results23 documents · clickable citations to Bates pages
flagged2 excerpts · paraphrase detected · verbatim shown side by side

The system distinguishes verbatim quotes from paraphrases. When the AI's draft paraphrases a quoted excerpt, both the AI's wording and the document's actual wording are displayed for the attorney to choose. Because citations come from the sealed retrieval index rather than parametric memory, fabricated case law is caught at the citation gate — every citation is traceable to a retrieved source, and the attorney verifies it before use.

The governance gate

⊘ SYSTEM REFUSES

Per the standard firm governance policy: Sentinel Node will not draft motions, briefs, pleadings, or any document intended for court filing. The argument is the lawyer's, not the system's. The system surfaces evidence, structures the timeline, retrieves precedent — the attorney writes the brief in their own voice using what the retrieval gathered.

The audit moment

▣ ENVELOPE CAPTURES

Every retrieved exhibit bound to its Bates-stamped source. Every paraphrase flagged as a paraphrase. The reviewing attorney's signoff on each citation before it enters the draft. If opposing counsel raises questions about the brief's evidence handling, the firm can produce the full retrieval-to-citation trail.
