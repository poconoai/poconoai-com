Sentinel Node Operational Walkthrough

What a pilot actually looks like, day by day.

Stakeholders keep asking: "What does Tuesday morning in a clinic look like after Pocono AI deploys?" This page answers that — concretely. No abstract capability claims, no roadmap slideware. Six phases, fixed checkpoints, explicit human-review procedures.

Start pilot intake → See the Day One guide
Day 0 Preparation

Site readiness call & one-page checklist

A 30-minute kickoff confirms admin contacts, document sources for first ingestion, and physical placement. Pocono AI sends a one-page checklist; nothing is shipped until the boxes are checked.

Site Readiness Checklist — Memo to Admin
  • [x] Designated clinical/legal admin: Dr. K. Patel
  • [x] Designated IT contact: J. Reyes (MSP)
  • [x] Physical placement: locked closet, Suite 204
  • [x] Power + ethernet drop confirmed
  • [ ] Document repositories identified (3 of 3 pending)
  • Kickoff call: 2026-05-19 10:00 ET
Day 1 Deployment

Hardware on-site, hardware-custody form signed

The Sentinel Node arrives pre-configured. On-site setup is ~90 minutes: physical placement, network connection, custody form. The device boots local-only; no cloud handshake required to operate. Pocono AI has zero remote access by default.

Hardware Custody Receipt — Sentinel-N-04217
  • Serial: SN-N-04217
  • Received by: Dr. K. Patel (designated admin)
  • Location: Suite 204, locked closet
  • Network mode: LAN-only, no WAN egress
  • Pocono AI remote access: disabled
  • Signature on file: 2026-05-21
Day 1–3 Ingestion

Documents ingested locally; nothing leaves the premises

Embeddings, the retrieval index, and the citation map are built on-device. A typical 5,000–25,000 document library completes in 24–48 hours, including OCR on scanned PDFs.

Ingestion Log — sentinel.local
  • [12:04:18] batch_001: 2,841 docs indexed
  • [14:22:01] OCR pass: 412 scanned PDFs
  • [15:18:44] citation map: page-level pointers built
  • [16:01:09] RBAC applied: 5 roles, 2,841 docs
  • [16:02:11] egress check: 0 bytes external
Day 3–5 Training

Staff onboarding & governance setup

Two training sessions: 60 minutes for end users (query interface, citation verification, escalation) and 45 minutes for admins (roles, audit logs, governance dashboard). Written governance policy template provided.

Staff Training Agenda — Session 1 of 2
  • 00:00 – 10:00  How retrieval works (no slides)
  • 10:00 – 25:00  Live walkthrough: query + citation verification
  • 25:00 – 40:00  The human-review queue — what reaches it, what doesn't
  • 40:00 – 55:00  When to escalate & how to log disagreement
  • 55:00 – 60:00  Q&A
Week 2–4 Validation

Mandatory human review on 100% of outputs

The first month runs in mandatory-review mode. Every AI-generated output passes through a licensed professional before it can be acted on. Disagreements are logged with reasons. Weekly check-in with Pocono AI to tune retrieval based on patterns.

Review Queue — Week 2 Summary
  • Outputs reviewed: 187 / 187 (100%)
  • Accepted as-written: 142
  • Edited before acceptance: 38
  • Rejected with logged reason: 7
  • Top rejection reason: citation mismatch (4 cases)
  • Action: retrieval rerank weight tuned, 2026-06-02
Ongoing Steady state

Local-only operation, quarterly health checks

After validation, the practice or firm sets its own review thresholds. Human-in-the-loop remains mandatory for any clinical or legal decision — that's a structural constraint, not a configuration. Quarterly health checks, on explicit request. Audit log retained on-device and exportable to existing systems.

Operations Dashboard — Q3 health check
  • Uptime (90d): 99.94%
  • Outputs reviewed (90d): 2,418 / 2,418 (100%)
  • Egress bytes (90d): 0
  • Audit log size: 218 MB (exportable)
  • Firmware patch level: 2026.08.1 (current)
What the pilot produces

Concrete deliverables, not slides

Every pilot ends with the same set of artifacts. These are the things your auditors, malpractice carriers, and compliance officers actually need to see — produced as a byproduct of operating the system, not constructed after the fact. See worked examples →

📄

Hardware custody chain

Signed receipt + location, network mode, and remote-access state for the duration of the pilot.
📚

Ingestion manifest

Per-document record: source, role-based access applied, citation pointers, OCR confidence.
✓

Review log

Every output, the reviewer, the decision (accept / edit / reject), and reasons for rejections.
🛡

Egress attestation

Daily byte-count of external network traffic. For a well-configured pilot, this is zero.
📊

Governance policy

One-page adopted policy defining prohibited uses, review coverage, and prohibited-decision categories.
🔍

Citation verification

Per-response report showing each cited claim, its location, and whether the claim is actually present.
