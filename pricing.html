After Contact

What happens after a pilot discovery request

Pilot admission is treated as an infrastructure engagement rather than a procurement transaction. The discovery sequence is structured around governance fit, workflow realism, and on-premises deployment feasibility. No call cadence, no follow-up automation, no sales sequence — the steps below are the entire process.

  1. 01

    Discovery conversation

    A 30–45 minute working conversation. We discuss the clinical or legal workflows under consideration, the document corpus involved, the current governance posture, and whether the practice's operational environment is a credible fit for an on-premises deployment. No deck. No demo. The call ends with a written summary of fit indicators.

  2. 02

    Infrastructure assessment

    For practices that proceed, a short technical assessment reviews network segmentation, existing IT relationships, MSP coverage, regional service access, physical placement for the appliance, and any pre-existing compliance frameworks. The output is a deployment feasibility memo — not a quote.

  3. 03

    Workflow review with clinical or legal leadership

    A focused working session with the licensed professionals whose workflows the system will support. We map the human-review chain, the documents that need to be retrievable, the situations where the system must abstain, and the boundary between AI assistance and licensed authorship. This is the conversation that determines whether the deployment will actually be used.

  4. 04

    Pilot qualification & regional fit

    Founding-cohort admission is reviewed against deployment capacity in the practice's region. We confirm we can support the deployment directly — named contacts, scheduled site visits where appropriate, and a defined escalation path — before any agreement is offered. Practices we cannot support to that standard are told so plainly.

  5. 05

    60-Day Paid Proof-of-Value engagement

    Qualified practices begin a structured 60-Day POV. This is operational deployment work — architecture, local RAG configuration, document sanitization pipeline, HITL workflow calibration — not a sales evaluation. The $5,000 fee applies toward implementation on conversion to a managed service agreement.

  6. 06

    Collaborative workflow refinement under supervised review

    Production use begins under supervised human review. Audit envelopes are inspected weekly. Retrieval is re-tuned as the document corpus and workflow evolve. Founding-cohort partners participate directly in the refinement loop — their operational feedback shapes the platform that subsequent cohorts inherit.

The discovery conversation through workflow review is non-binding. No agreement, no engagement fee, and no obligation arises until both sides choose to proceed to the 60-Day POV.

Infrastructure Longevity

Infrastructure Lifecycle Management

AI inference hardware has a meaningful performance ceiling at roughly two years. GPU VRAM requirements, quantization options, and model size demands evolve faster than standard server refresh cycles. Pocono AI manages this lifecycle proactively — the infrastructure refresh is built into the managed service agreement, not negotiated as an add-on at renewal.

Managed Infrastructure Refresh at Month 24

Every HaaS Standard and Rent-to-Own contract includes a hardware refresh at month 24 at no additional charge. A new Sentinel Node pre-imaged to the current golden image is delivered to your site. Your encrypted data drives are physically transferred to the new chassis during a scheduled overnight maintenance window. Your data never leaves the building.

Overnight Swap Execution

The swap is executed by a Pocono AI-certified MSP partner during a maintenance window. The new unit boots, validates the TPM 2.0 measured boot chain, and comes online. The old chassis is returned with drives removed. Drives are wiped to NIST 800-88 standards after you confirm the new unit is operational. Total on-site time is typically two to four hours.

⚙ Outright Purchase Path

For capital purchase arrangements, an infrastructure refresh at month 24 is available. We will notify you at month 18 with current configuration options. No deployment is left running an obsolete configuration without a clear path forward.

Common Questions

How does infrastructure deployment work?

Pocono AI deploys and manages dedicated AI infrastructure within your office environment. Standard deployments are fully managed — infrastructure is Pocono AI-maintained with exclusive client-side custody throughout the engagement. An ownership track (+$150/month toward title transfer) and capital purchase arrangements are available on request. In all cases, your data stays physically within your building and you retain full data ownership and export rights on exit.

What happens if I cancel early?

The 24-month contract term is binding. If you cancel before the end of term, you must either return the hardware in good condition within 10 days of termination notice, or pay 50% of the remaining monthly payments owed under the contract. The $5,000 implementation fee is non-refundable in all cases. On exit, you receive a full data export of all indexed documents and audit logs before the hardware is retrieved. Encrypted drives are NIST 800-88 purged only after you confirm receipt of your data export.

Is the monthly fee tax-deductible?

Monthly HaaS fees are structured as operating expenses, not capital expenditures — typically deductible in the year incurred (no depreciation schedule). Consult your tax advisor for your specific situation.

What does the implementation timeline look like?

From signed POV agreement to first productive inference: 2–4 weeks for most independent practices. This includes hardware procurement, VLAN configuration, RAG pipeline setup, and HITL workflow calibration with your team.

Do you work with our MSP?

Yes. We've built an MSP Partner Program specifically for this. Your existing IT provider can be involved at the referral or installation level. See the Partners page for program details.

What models run on the Sentinel Node?

Current deployment stack: Ollama as the inference engine, running Gemma and Llama 3/4 variants. Model selection is tuned at deployment for your use case. All models run fully locally — no API calls to any cloud provider.

Pricing Philosophy

Why We Structured Pricing This Way

A note on current pricing: The rates published here represent Phase 1 Founding Deployment pricing — intentionally structured for early pilot partners during the initial infrastructure rollout phase. Pricing is expected to evolve as deployment capacity, infrastructure scope, and enterprise feature sets expand. Early deployment partners who establish a managed service agreement during the founding phase lock in their rate for the full initial term.
🔒

Local custody from day one

Regardless of deployment structure, the Sentinel Node lives physically in your building under your exclusive operational custody. Pocono AI cannot access the hardware remotely without your consent. Zero telemetry, no cloud, and data sovereignty are architectural properties of the deployment — not contractual promises that depend on who holds the title.

📈

Lower long-term cost than per-seat cloud licensing

Per-seat AI licensing from major vendors escalates annually. A five-provider practice paying $150–$300 per seat per month crosses $9,000–$18,000 annually — before add-ons, storage overages, or compliance audits. Our flat monthly managed infrastructure rate does not escalate with headcount or query volume.

⚙️

No recurring licensing escalation

Cloud AI vendors renegotiate pricing at renewal. Model API costs fluctuate with provider infrastructure decisions. Because inference runs on locally deployed infrastructure within your office, your operational cost is not subject to upstream cloud vendor pricing changes.

📋

Transparent, predictable costs

One implementation fee. One monthly rate. No usage tiers, no API metering, no storage overage charges. The total cost of ownership is calculable from day one. We publish it above, not in a sales call.

🏙️

Local data sovereignty — by architecture

PHI and privileged client records never traverse an external network during inference. This is an architectural property, not a policy commitment. It cannot be reversed by a vendor update, an acquisition, or a terms-of-service change.

⚡

Deployment-first, not demo-first

The implementation fee funds real deployment work: infrastructure configuration, network architecture, document pipeline setup, and HITL workflow calibration specific to your practice. It is non-refundable because the work is real. The structured POV engagement generates validated results before any long-term commitment is made. The POV is a structured engagement, not a free trial.

Architecture & Oversight

Human Oversight Is Not Optional

The Sentinel Node is an AI-assisted retrieval and drafting tool. It is not an autonomous decision system. Every workflow is designed with a mandatory human review step before any clinical, legal, or compliance-sensitive output is used. This is not a disclaimer — it is the architecture.

🧜

AI Retrieves & Drafts

The system retrieves relevant documents, assembles context, and produces cited draft outputs. Every statement is traceable to a source document and page.

👁️

Humans Review & Validate

A licensed professional reviews, edits, and approves every output before it enters a clinical note, legal document, or patient communication. The AI does not act unilaterally.

📋

Every Action Logged Locally

A 90-day append-only HMAC audit log captures every retrieval, every output, and every human approval — on-device, under your control. Available for compliance review on demand.

🚩

Calibrated Per Workflow

Human-in-the-Loop thresholds are calibrated during deployment to match your specific workflows — prior auth, note drafting, document search, or billing support. Not a one-size template.

ⓘ  The Sentinel Node does not provide medical advice, legal advice, or compliance certification. All clinical and legal outputs require review and validation by an appropriately licensed professional before use. Technology deployment does not constitute compliance with HIPAA, ABA Rules, or any other regulatory framework — that responsibility remains with your practice and its licensed personnel.
