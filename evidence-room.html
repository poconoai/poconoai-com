Attack a working audit chain.

Since the moment this page opened, it has been keeping a record of your visit — each act sealed with SHA-256 and chained to the act before it, entirely inside your own browser. Our whole company rests on the claim that a record like this can be trusted. So we will not ask you to believe it.

We invite you to attack it. Rewrite history. Cover your tracks. Forge the entire chain. Each attack works a little — and each one is caught by a different layer of custody. Synthetic data only.       How this page is built — the fine print
      • Hashing. SHA-256 via the browser’s Web Crypto API, with a self-contained pure-JavaScript fallback (verified against published test vectors) for non-secure contexts. Each record’s seal covers its sequence number, timestamp, actor, event, detail, and the previous record’s hash.
      • Storage. An append-only array in page memory. No cookies, no localStorage, no sessionStorage, no IndexedDB, no network requests, no analytics. Reload the page and the chain is gone — deliberately.
      • The three detection layers you just used: content seals catch edits; link integrity catches re-sealed records; witness anchors catch full forgeries. Production custody adds reviewer identity, policy gates, and packet export — this room demonstrates the geometry, not the whole machine.
      • Accessibility. Keyboard operable throughout, visible focus, 44px touch targets, status changes announced politely, color never the only signal, prefers-reduced-motion honored. Audio is optional, user-initiated, and duplicated in text.
      • See for yourself. View source — the entire room is one static page.

      The Evidence Room is an educational demonstration using synthetic data only. It is not the Sentinel Node product, not a security audit, and not a representation that any system is immune to compromise — tamper-evidence is about detection and accountability, not invulnerability.       Audit & Security Roadmap Reviewable today Tamper-evident next

      Reviewable Today. Tamper-Evident Next.

      Sentinel Node’s pilot architecture preserves a reviewable audit trail for approved-source retrieval, draft generation, human review, and go-live reporting. We do not claim that citations prove correctness, or that a local log is tamper-proof. Our next audit-layer release adds hash-chained audit events, signed verification bundles, admin-action logging, and optional off-box/WORM retention for stronger tamper-evidence.

      Citations support human review — they do not prove the AI’s interpretation is correct. Sentinel Node produces cited draft output from approved sources; a qualified person reviews before any clinical, legal, or operational use. “Tamper-evident” here means alteration is detectable, not impossible.
