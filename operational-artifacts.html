Sentinel Node Operational Realism

What auditors actually see.

Every pilot produces the same six artifacts as a byproduct of normal operation. Each one is the format your compliance officers, malpractice carriers, IT directors, and external auditors actually receive — not a marketing summary about the format, but a worked example of the document itself.

●
Synthetic data notice. All identifiers, hashes, query strings, document references, and patient/case names below are fabricated for illustration. Field structure, log format, and policy language match what real deployments produce; the values do not.

1

Reviewable audit log (hash-chained: Stage 2)

JSONL export

Every prompt, retrieval, response, and review decision is appended to a reviewable audit log. Each entry references the previous entry's hash, so any retroactive edit breaks the chain and is immediately detectable. Logs export as line-delimited JSON for ingestion into your SIEM or compliance system.

What they verify: chain integrity (no missing sequence numbers, every prev_hash matches the prior entry's chain_hash), reviewer attribution on every AI-produced output, and rejection reasons aligned with policy.
2

Document ingestion manifest

CSV export

For every document the system ingests, the manifest records source, role-based access controls applied, OCR confidence (for scans), citation pointer depth, and the ingestion timestamp. Exported as CSV for spreadsheet review.

Who reads this: practice administrators verifying role assignments, IT confirming source provenance, and the deployment lead reviewing low-OCR-confidence scans flagged for re-scan. 3

Human review queue summary

Weekly PDF

Generated weekly by the system. Lists every AI output produced in the period, the reviewing clinician or attorney, the decision, and rejection reasons aggregated by pattern. This is the document that most often goes to a malpractice carrier or state-bar audit.

Every AI output flows through human review; all four outcomes are appended to the reviewable audit log.

Who reads this: the practice or firm's principal, malpractice carrier annual review, state bar / state medical board on request. What they verify: 100% review coverage (no AI output bypassed a human), rejection reasons match policy, and action items get followed up week-over-week.
4

Citation verification report

Per-response

Attached to every AI response. Lists each citation, the exact document and page-range, a verification status (citation actually appears in the referenced location, or it doesn't), and the embedding-similarity score of the retrieved chunk to the query. This is the most-used artifact during human review.

Response cites 3 sources: [1] doc_4198 prescribing_guidelines_v7.docx Page range: pp. 14–15 Verification: ✓ Claim located on p. 14, ¶3 Embed similarity: 0.847 Reviewer note: accepted [2] doc_4172 progress_notes_2024.pdf Page range: p. 41 Verification: ✓ Patient history confirms renal status Embed similarity: 0.792 Reviewer note: accepted [3] doc_4205 vitals_export_q1.pdf Page range: p. 3 Verification: ⚠ Page contains creatinine value but does NOT mention dosing interval Embed similarity: 0.681 Reviewer note: cited in error — removed from final ────────────────────────────────────────────── Reviewer decision: accept_with_edit Edit: removed citation [3]; retained [1] and [2] as basis. Edit distance: 18 chars ──────────────────────────────────────────────
Who reads this: the reviewing clinician or attorney, during the review step. Also stored alongside the response in the audit log for downstream audit. What they verify: every cited claim actually appears in the cited location. Citations marked ⚠ are the most common rejection trigger and the single biggest argument for mandatory human review.
5

Network egress attestation

Daily report

The Sentinel Node logs every byte of outbound network traffic and attests daily to its external footprint. For a well-configured deployment (no WAN egress allowed), this is zero bytes outbound for the lifetime of the device.

Who reads this: IT, security officers, HIPAA compliance, defense counsel responding to data-breach allegations. What they verify: the zero in row 1. Any nonzero WAN outbound is grounds for immediate investigation. The signed attestation entries into the daily reviewable audit log (hash chaining is the Stage 2 audit-layer) so the zero-egress claim is itself reviewable.
6

Governance policy (adopted)

Template + adopted

Pocono AI provides a one-page governance policy template at deployment. The practice or firm adopts it, edits where appropriate, signs it, and stores it as the binding policy document. This is the artifact that defines which decisions the AI may inform and which remain unconditionally human.

ai-governance-policy-v1.0.pdfadopted 2026-05-20
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════ AI GOVERNANCE POLICY — Suite 204 Family Medicine Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-05-20 ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════ SECTION 1. SCOPE The Sentinel Node deployed at this practice is a retrieval-augmented drafting tool. It produces drafts of progress notes, prior-auth language, and patient-instruction text. It does not place orders, prescribe medications, or generate clinical decisions. SECTION 2. HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP Every AI-generated output is reviewed by a licensed clinician before any clinical action is taken. Review coverage is 100% and this constraint is structurally enforced — review cannot be disabled by configuration. SECTION 3. PROHIBITED USES The AI system MUST NOT be used to: (a) issue medication orders without clinician review (b) generate billing codes without clinician review (c) communicate clinical guidance directly to patients (d) make eligibility, scheduling, or triage decisions that affect access to care SECTION 4. AUDIT Every prompt, retrieval, response, and review decision is logged in a reviewable audit log retained 90 days minimum. Logs are exportable to the practice's compliance system. SECTION 5. DATA No patient data leaves the premises. The Sentinel Node operates local-only. WAN egress is structurally blocked and verified daily via signed attestation. SECTION 6. REVIEW AND AMENDMENT This policy is reviewed quarterly by the practice principal and amended as needed. Amendments require signature and are appended with version history. ────────────────────────────────────────────── Signed: Dr. K. Patel, Principal 2026-05-20 J. Reyes, IT/Compliance Officer 2026-05-20 ──────────────────────────────────────────────
Who reads this: the practice principal during adoption, board of directors at quarterly review, state medical board / state bar in any inquiry, malpractice carrier annually. What they verify: the prohibited-uses list (sec. 3) is enforced — any audit-log entry showing AI involvement in a prohibited category is a governance breach and triggers internal review.
