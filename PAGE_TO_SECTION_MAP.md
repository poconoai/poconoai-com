# Page-to-Section Map — Pocono AI v350 IA Restructure

new top-level navigation, plus optional **secondary** sections where the page is
legitimately reachable from more than one buyer journey. It is the source of truth
for the v350 header dropdowns, footer columns, and grouped sitemap.

Tools & Demos · Evidence · Investors · Company.
