Sentinel Node Governance Review · Operational Walkthrough

What governance looks like on a working screen.

Most AI vendors describe governance as a policy. Pocono AI implements it as a sequence of visible review states a clinician or attorney sees, touches, and signs. This page shows those states the way they appear in the Sentinel Node review interface — with the same labels, the same evidence cards, and the same audit entries that get written to disk.

These mockups are representative of the production review surface. They are not marketing visuals. Every state below corresponds to a checkpoint that the system refuses to skip.

01 · Evidence Retrieved 02 · Draft — Not Final 03 · Human Review Required 04 · Citation Gate Blocked 05 · Audit Record Generated 06 · Chain of Custody

01 · Evidence Retrieved

Before the model is allowed to draft, the retrieval layer surfaces the specific passages it will be permitted to use. Nothing the model writes can refer to a source that does not appear in this panel.

SENTINEL · RETRIEVAL · CASE 4471
node-A · local
Evidence Retrieved 4 passages from 2 sources · cosine ≥ 0.78 · rerank applied

Clinician question

What does the payer’s 2025 medical policy say about prior authorization for advanced cardiac MRI in symptomatic patients with non-ischemic cardiomyopathy?

Retrieved evidence (the model may cite only these)

[1]
“Cardiac MRI is considered medically necessary for the evaluation of suspected non-ischemic cardiomyopathy when echocardiogram findings are inconclusive…” policies / 2025-cardiac-mri-medical-policy.pdf · p.4 · §3.2.1
0.91
[2]
“Documentation must include symptom onset, prior cardiology consultation, and explicit statement that echocardiogram findings were non-diagnostic.” policies / 2025-cardiac-mri-medical-policy.pdf · p.5 · §3.2.4
0.87
[3]
“Patients with documented symptoms persisting beyond six weeks and prior negative non-invasive evaluation qualify under the symptomatic pathway.” policies / 2025-cardiac-mri-medical-policy.pdf · p.6 · §3.3
0.82
[4]
“Appeals submitted with reference to the specific policy section in dispute are processed under the expedited clinical review pathway.” payer-guides / appeals-procedure-2025.pdf · p.2
0.78

Why this matters. If the model later writes a sentence that cannot be traced to one of these four passages, the citation gate (state 04) refuses to release it.

02 · Draft — Not Final

The model produces a draft. Every clinical claim carries a citation pointing back to the evidence panel. Nothing is queued, sent, or committed until a human signs off.

SENTINEL · DRAFT · CASE 4471
not final · not sent
Draft — Not Final 4 citations · 0 uncited claims · awaiting human sign-off

Drafted appeal letter (excerpt)

This appeal is submitted on behalf of patient [redacted], for prior authorization denial of advanced cardiac MRI.

Per the payer’s own 2025 medical policy, cardiac MRI is considered medically necessary for evaluation of suspected non-ischemic cardiomyopathy when echocardiogram findings are inconclusive[1]. The policy further specifies that documentation must include symptom onset, prior cardiology consultation, and a statement that echocardiogram findings were non-diagnostic[2] — criteria met in this case.

Symptoms have persisted beyond six weeks, with prior negative non-invasive evaluation, placing this patient within the symptomatic pathway defined in [3]. We respectfully request expedited clinical review under the appeals procedure[4].

The draft is inert until a human acts on it. There is no auto-send queue. There is no “background submission.” The draft sits in this state and waits.

03 · Human Review Required

When the system detects a condition that warrants higher scrutiny — ambiguous evidence, low retrieval confidence, conflict between two policies, missing source — the review state escalates and the reviewer is shown why.

SENTINEL · REVIEW · CASE 4472
escalated
Human Review Required Two policy sources retrieved — reviewer must reconcile.

Reason for escalation

Retrieval surfaced two passages that conflict on coverage criteria. The model produced a draft, but flagged the conflict and refused to silently pick a side. A clinician must decide which source governs this case before the draft can be signed.

Conflicting evidence

[A]
Coverage is limited to patients with prior cardiology consultation within 90 days. policies / 2025-cardiac-mri-medical-policy.pdf · §3.2.4
0.86
[B]
The 90-day requirement is waived when the referring physician provides an attestation of acute symptom progression. payer-bulletins / Q3-2025-updates.pdf · p.7
0.79

The model does not resolve conflicts on its own. It surfaces them, and asks. The reviewer’s choice is recorded in the audit trail along with their identity and the timestamp.

04 · Citation Gate Blocked

The system inspects every claim in the draft. If any sentence cannot be traced to a retrieved passage, the draft is held. The reviewer never sees the unsupported text presented as fact.

SENTINEL · GATE · CASE 4473
held
Citation Gate Blocked 1 uncited claim detected · draft suppressed from reviewer queue

Suppressed sentence

“The patient’s presentation is consistent with stage 2 cardiomyopathy as defined in the 2024 cardiology guidelines.”

Why it was held

No passage matching “2024 cardiology guidelines” was present in the retrieved evidence. The model attempted to assert a source that does not exist in this case’s document set. The sentence is logged and the draft is reissued without it.

This is the mechanism that prevents the hallucination from reaching a clinician’s eyes. The block is logged. The attempt is logged. The corrective action is logged.

05 · Audit Record Generated

When the reviewer signs, a sealed audit record is written. It captures the question, the evidence used, the draft, the reviewer’s identity, the action taken, the timestamp, and the cryptographic hash that ties this record to the one before it.

The record is append-only. Each entry hashes the previous entry. Tampering with any record invalidates every record written after it. The chain stays on the appliance.

06 · Chain of Custody, End to End

Each of the previous five states writes one or more entries. Together they form a continuous chain — from the question that entered the node, through every retrieval, every model output, every gate decision, every reviewer action.

01

Question recorded

The clinician’s prompt is hashed and stored. The PHI body stays in the local case record; the audit only carries the hash.

writes: question_hash, case_id, node_id
02

Retrieval pinned

Each retrieved passage is hashed and recorded with source, page, and section. The model cannot later cite anything that is not in this list.

writes: evidence[] with per-source hashes
03

Draft generated and gated

The draft is produced. Every claim is verified against the pinned evidence. The gate either passes the draft to the reviewer or holds it and logs the suppression reason.

writes: draft_hash, citation_gate, suppressions[]
04

Reviewer action captured

Approve, edit, or reject. The reviewer’s identity and the action they took are recorded. Edits produce a diff entry alongside the original draft hash.

writes: reviewer, action, edit_diff?
05

Record sealed and linked

The record is hashed, signed with a node-bound key, and linked to the previous record by hash. The chain extends. The receipt is now reproducible from the appliance alone.

writes: this_hash, prev_hash, sig, signed_at

Why this is not what cloud AI does

The states above are not a layer added on top of a cloud model. They are the operating posture of the node. Removing any one of them breaks the chain. The contrast is direct:

Cloud AI workflow

  • Prompt and PHI leave the practice perimeter on every request.
  • Retrieval is opaque or absent — the model decides what to cite.
  • No native citation gate. Unsupported claims surface as confident prose.
  • “Human in the loop” is a setting, not a state the system refuses to leave.
  • Audit logs are vendor-side, partial, and produced for the vendor’s telemetry — not for the regulator at your door.
  • Retention, sub-processors, and model updates change without your sign-off.

Sentinel Node workflow

  • Prompt and PHI never leave the appliance. Zero external egress.
  • Retrieval is pinned. Every passage is hashed and shown before drafting.
  • Citation gate is mandatory. Uncited claims are suppressed and logged.
  • Human review is a state. The system cannot ship a draft past it.
  • The audit chain is local, append-only, hash-linked, and reproducible from the box.
  • Model and policy updates are signed bundles you accept — or don’t.
