Zero-Telemetry AI Infrastructure for Healthcare

Most healthcare AI tools quietly send usage data — including the text of your queries — back to a vendor. In an exam room, those queries contain protected health information. Sentinel Node is built so that no query content or usage telemetry is routed off your network: inference runs locally, and the only record is the one on your own audit log.

Language note: “Zero-telemetry” describes the technical architecture of Sentinel Node — it is a design property, not a HIPAA certification. Practices remain responsible for their own compliance determinations. Human review remains the gatekeeper on AI-assisted output. This page focuses on healthcare; a companion page covers the concept across healthcare and law.

Who this is for

  • Independent practices and clinics evaluating AI under HIPAA-sensitive constraints.
  • Practice owners who want to know exactly what an AI tool transmits before they adopt it.
  • Compliance-minded clinicians wary of cloud tools that log query content.

The problem

Cloud AI systems routinely collect usage telemetry: the text of every prompt, the response, timestamps, and session metadata. Vendors use it for model improvement, abuse detection, and diagnostics. In general business use that is unremarkable. In healthcare, the prompt is often a patient's data — and the telemetry pathway sends it to a third party, frequently without clear disclosure at the moment of use.

How Pocono AI approaches it

Sentinel Node runs an open-weight model on a dedicated appliance inside the practice network. When staff submit a query, it is processed entirely on local hardware and the response is returned over the internal network. No query content, patient data, or usage telemetry is routed to external endpoints. The local Audit Proxy logs each query in a reviewable audit record so accountability does not depend on cloud storage.

A realistic workflow: a chart summary request

  1. A clinician asks Sentinel Node to summarize a patient's recent visit history before an appointment.
  2. The request is processed on the local appliance; the patient's data is never transmitted to a vendor.
  3. The model returns a cited summary and flags anything ambiguous in the record rather than smoothing it over.
  4. The query and the clinician's review are written to the local audit log — the only record of the interaction, held by the practice.

Why zero-telemetry matters in healthcare specifically

The difference between a cloud AI tool and a zero-telemetry one is not a setting — it is the architecture. A cloud tool's default is to transmit and retain query content; a zero-telemetry appliance never sends it in the first place. In healthcare, where the query is so often protected health information, that distinction determines whether using AI creates a new exposure pathway or closes one.

Human review and the audit trail

Sentinel Node returns AI output as a draft, never a decision. A qualified person reviews before anything is relied on, and the language model is never called directly — every interaction passes through an Audit Proxy that records the query, the retrieved sources, the output, and the human sign-off into a reviewable local audit log. That keeps the record reviewable and in your custody rather than a vendor cloud.

What Pocono AI does not claim

  • We do not claim zero-telemetry equals HIPAA compliance — compliance is broader than any one architectural property.
  • We do not claim the AI is a clinical decision-maker; outputs are reviewable drafts.
  • We do not claim zero risk — we claim a smaller, locally controlled breach surface.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is “telemetry” in an AI tool?

Telemetry is the usage data a system sends back to its vendor — including prompt text, responses, timestamps, and session metadata. In healthcare AI, that prompt text often contains protected health information.

Does Sentinel Node send any telemetry?

By default, no query content, patient data, or usage telemetry is routed to external endpoints. The only optional outbound connection is signed update verification, which can be disabled in air-gapped deployments.

Does this make my practice HIPAA compliant?

No single product can. Zero-telemetry architecture supports HIPAA-sensitive work by removing an exposure pathway, but your practice remains responsible for its own risk analysis and compliance program.

How do I know nothing is being sent?

The architecture runs inference locally and can operate air-gapped, and the local audit log records every query. The architecture and security pages document the data flow for technical review.

Is there any record of what the AI did?

Yes — a reviewable audit log held locally by your practice. That is the record, and it stays under your control rather than in a vendor's cloud.

Does this website use any external services?

The zero-telemetry property above describes the Sentinel Node product. The public website keeps the same posture: no hidden tracking pixels, advertising trackers, or behavioral analytics. The only intentional external services are YouTube for video content and Google Forms for voluntary inquiries (the interest list), which opens in a new tab only if you choose to click it. Please do not submit private medical records, legal files, confidential client materials, or sensitive documents through the form.
