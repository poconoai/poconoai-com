What This Is

Not a promise to fix people.

Rehability is not a cure, a clinical treatment, or a replacement for professional care. It is a commitment to build tools that give people more usable days — practical local AI support for the workflows that get harder when life is hard.

Local-first, private by design

Your notes, symptoms, and documents stay on your device. No cloud. No third-party servers. No one reading your pain logs or appointment prep.

User-controlled, always

You decide what gets captured, what gets shared, and what gets exported. Every tool requires your explicit action. Nothing leaves without your review.

Drafts, not decisions

Every Rehability tool produces a draft. You — and any professional you involve — make the actual decisions. Human review is built in, not optional.

Built from lived experience

These are not tools imagined by a healthy product team. They exist because someone who lives with disability needed them and built them. That matters.

Plugin-based structure

Each tool is bounded, purposeful, and independent. New tools can be added without turning the whole product into a fragile monolith. Small tools matter when life is hard.

Accessibility-forward UI

Large tap targets. High contrast. Plain language. Print-friendly output. Screen-reader labels. Designed for people who may be lying down, in pain, or using dictation.

Why Plugins

Small tools. No fragile monolith.

Sentinel Home / Rehability is designed to support more tools over time without growing into an unmaintainable sprawl. Each plugin follows the same four rules.

Rule 1

Bounded purpose

Each tool does one thing well. Pain capture captures pain. Appointment prep prepares for appointments. A tool that tries to do everything does nothing reliably.

Rule 2

Local-first where possible

No network calls during a tool run unless the user has explicitly approved them. Your pain notes don't need to leave your device to be useful to you.

Rule 3

User-controlled and privacy-preserving

The user decides what's captured, what's exported, and what's shared. Tools don't make decisions on their own. Human review is required on every export.

Rule 4

Draft-only output

Rehability tools produce drafts, summaries, and support materials. They do not diagnose, prescribe, advise legally, or make final professional decisions.
