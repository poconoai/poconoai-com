Coexistence · Not Replacement

Works with your current systems.

Small law firms don't fear inefficiency. They fear disruption. The matter management system that took two years to standardize, the network drive every paralegal knows by heart, the Outlook archive that goes back to 2015 — these are the things a firm cannot afford to break. Sentinel Node is designed to coexist with them, not replace them.

The premise: Sentinel Node reads from the documents your firm already keeps, in the places your firm already keeps them. It does not require you to migrate to a new case management system, abandon Outlook, restructure your file shares, or re-paper your client relationships. Your stack stays. The retrieval layer is added underneath it.

The systems Sentinel Node coexists with.

What this looks like for the platforms most small firms actually run.

PDF

Scanned PDFs & Network Shares

Reads · Indexes

The bulk of small-firm institutional knowledge lives in scanned documents and network file shares. Sentinel Node ingests these in place — through OCR, structure detection, and entity extraction — without requiring you to move files into a new system.

What you keep doing: save documents where you save them today.

OL

Outlook & Email Archives

Reads · Indexes

The email chain where opposing counsel confirmed a deadline. The PDF attachment that became Exhibit C. The settlement offer buried in the firm's archive. Sentinel Node indexes the firm's email archive locally so retrieval can surface what's needed without forwarding, downloading, or restructuring.

What you keep doing: use Outlook exactly as you do now.

CL

Clio

Coexists · Reads

If your firm runs on Clio, Sentinel Node coexists with it. Matters, contacts, billing entries, and document associations from Clio remain the source of truth. Sentinel Node reads from Clio's data layer for context — it does not replace Clio's matter management.

What you keep doing: manage matters in Clio. Bill from Clio. Run the firm from Clio.

MC

MyCase

Coexists · Reads

Same posture as Clio. MyCase remains the practice management system. Sentinel Node reads matter context and document associations from MyCase for retrieval purposes. Your billing, calendaring, and client portal stay where they are.

What you keep doing: use MyCase as your day-to-day system of record.

iM

iManage

Coexists · Reads

For firms running iManage as their document management system, Sentinel Node reads from the iManage repository under the firm's existing permissions model. Document versioning, check-in/check-out, and matter-level security stay with iManage. Sentinel Node provides the retrieval layer on top.

What you keep doing: iManage remains the system of record for documents.

SP

SharePoint

Reads · Indexes

If your firm uses SharePoint as a document repository, Sentinel Node indexes the relevant document libraries while respecting SharePoint's permission boundaries. Documents stay in SharePoint. Retrieval happens through Sentinel Node.

What you keep doing: use SharePoint for documents and team collaboration.

PD

Existing PDF & Document Repositories

Imports · Indexes

Years of accumulated PDFs, Word documents, and scanned exhibits — sitting in whatever folder structure the firm built up over time. Sentinel Node ingests these where they live, builds a searchable index, and preserves the original file structure unchanged.

What you keep doing: nothing different. The files stay put.

QB

Billing & Accounting Systems

Coexists

QuickBooks, Tabs3, TimeSolv, PCLaw — whatever your firm uses for billing and accounting remains untouched. Sentinel Node does not handle financial transactions, billing entries, or accounting records.

What you keep doing: your bookkeeper runs the books exactly as before.

⚠ What we're being honest about.

Integration depth varies by platform. Sentinel Node has tighter native integration with some systems (Outlook, SharePoint, file shares, scanned PDFs) than others. Integration with Clio, MyCase, and iManage depends on each platform's API availability and the firm's licensing tier. We will tell you the integration ceiling for your specific stack during the paid Proof-of-Value scoping — not after.

We are not a Clio competitor, an iManage competitor, or a SharePoint competitor. Sentinel Node sits underneath those systems as a retrieval and governance layer. If a vendor pitches you "the AI that replaces your case management system," that vendor is selling a different category of product, and the migration risk is much higher.

Some legacy systems will need a bridge. If your firm runs a 2008 vintage document management system or a proprietary in-house tool, integration may require a custom connector during the pilot. We will scope this transparently rather than promise out-of-the-box compatibility we can't deliver.

Gradual adoption. No rip-and-replace.

How Sentinel Node enters a firm without disrupting day-to-day operations.

PHASE 01

Read-only retrieval over existing repositories

Sentinel Node indexes the firm's existing PDFs, Outlook archive, and case management system. The firm uses retrieval for one or two matter types. Nothing changes about how documents are created or stored.

Weeks 1–4

PHASE 02

Workflow augmentation in selected practices

The firm starts using Sentinel Node for intake worksheets, conflict checks, and discovery review in selected practice areas. The case management system remains the system of record. Attorneys decide what gets adopted next.

Weeks 5–8

PHASE 03

Audit chain and governance enforcement

Full audit envelope chain is active across all AI-assisted work. The firm's governance policy is enforced at the system level. The pilot evaluation period closes. The firm decides whether Sentinel Node stays.

Weeks 9–12

What we promise about your existing systems.

The specific commitments we make during deployment.

  • Your file structure stays intact. Sentinel Node reads from where your documents live today. It does not require you to reorganize folders, rename files, or migrate to a new repository.
  • Your case management system remains the system of record. Clio, MyCase, iManage, or whatever you run — that stays. Sentinel Node sits underneath, not on top.
  • Outlook stays Outlook. No mailbox migration, no new email client, no plug-in that takes over your inbox.
  • Your billing system is not touched. Financial records, time entries, and invoicing stay in whatever system you use today.
  • Your existing IT vendor or MSP stays in the loop. Sentinel Node deploys to your environment with your IT partner's involvement — not around them.
  • If we hit a system we can't integrate with, we tell you up front. Not after the pilot starts. Not after the contract is signed. During scoping.
