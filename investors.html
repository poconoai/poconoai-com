Pricing Model

Hardware-as-a-Service. Recurring revenue from day one. All tiers include on-site installation, BAA v3.0 (draft, for review by your counsel), 90-day onboarding, and hardware refresh at month 24. $5,000 non-refundable implementation fee on all tiers. Minimum service term: 24 months. Pocono AI deploys and manages dedicated AI infrastructure within your practice environment through a predictable monthly managed service rate. Hardware remains under Pocono AI management throughout the engagement; clients receive exclusive on-premises custody for the duration of the service term. Customer data sovereignty: all data residing on the practice's encrypted SSDs is the client's property at every stage of the relationship. On hardware refresh or service termination, client SSDs containing practice data are retained by the client or destroyed at client direction; only Pocono AI-owned compute hardware is returned. Early termination: return Pocono AI hardware within 10 days or pay 50% of remaining contract payments.

Current pricing reflects Phase 1 Founding Deployment partner rates during the initial rollout phase. Pricing is structured to drive early adoption and validate deployment infrastructure. Rates are expected to increase in future enterprise deployment phases as deployment capacity, managed service scope, and enterprise feature sets expand. Founding deployment partners are grandfathered at their contracted rate for the full initial term.

Risks & Mitigations

⚠️ Key Person Risk
Risk: Marcus is currently the sole technical lead. Mitigation: Four-layer continuity designed in from the start: (1) Dell ProSupport 4-hour on-site hardware response operates 24×7 independently of Pocono AI personnel; (2) every deployment includes a documented self-service recovery protocol so practice staff can restore operations without founder involvement; (3) Junior DBA hire funded from pre-seed angel round as first post-capital hire; (4) fractional CTO engagement in progress for architecture review and bus-factor redundancy. Customer practices cannot be stranded by a single person's availability.
⚠️ Sales Cycle & Channel
Risk: Healthcare sales cycles can stretch 6–12 months. Mitigation: 3 MSPs in Q3 2026 discussion for managed deployment. Direct PA/NJ/NY sales concurrent. Pilot revenue de-risks before scale.
⚠️ Hardware Supply Chain
Risk: GPU availability could constrain deployments. Mitigation: Dell partnership with ProSupport 4-hour onsite. $240K hardware inventory from pre-seed angel round. Encrypted daily backup for business continuity.
⚠️ Regulatory Compliance
Risk: HIPAA enforcement changes or new requirements. Mitigation: SOC 2 Type I audit and technology E&O / cyber-liability insurance are planned and funded by this round. BAA reviewed by healthcare counsel. The local-first, audit-first architecture is designed so that tighter regulation increases, rather than erodes, Pocono's value.
