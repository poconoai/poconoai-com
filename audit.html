Third-party iframes — present, disclosed

Five pages embed YouTube video iframes for product demonstration content:

  • index.html
  • for-attorneys.html
  • for-physicians.html
  • governance.html
  • sentinel-demo.html

These iframes use youtube.com/embed/ URLs. When a visitor scrolls to the section containing the iframe, the browser will load the YouTube embed from Google’s servers. This means YouTube can observe visitor IP, user-agent, and referrer for visitors who view those pages. The iframes are present because the demo videos are hosted on YouTube. If you prefer to avoid that exposure, do not interact with the embedded player and do not scroll into the embed region; using a privacy-respecting browser extension or YouTube’s youtube-nocookie.com variant would further reduce the exposure. Each YouTube embed now carries a visible third-party disclosure notice. YouTube is the only third-party dependency on this site. We continue to evaluate migration to youtube-nocookie.com in a future release.

Outbound links

The site contains outbound href= citation links to academic sources (arxiv.org, aclanthology.org), product documentation (LanceDB, Pinecone, Weaviate, etc.), GitHub repositories, government sources (HHS, federal court records), and news outlets. These are user-initiated navigation links. They are not loaded automatically and do not transmit data unless a visitor clicks them.

Cookies and storage

The site sets no cookies. localStorage and sessionStorage are used by nav.js and pilot-cta.js to remember banner dismissals (e.g. so the pilot cohort notice stays dismissed across sessions). These are first-party, browser-local, and contain only boolean flags. No personal information is stored.
