Sentinel Node Pilot Evidence Framework

The proof layer before testimonials exist.

Sentinel Node is early. Pocono AI should not fake customer proof, inflate ROI, or imply clinical validation it has not earned. This framework shows exactly what a paid Proof-of-Value is designed to measure: workflow burden, retrieval quality, review behavior, audit completeness, and expansion readiness.

What the pilot is supposed to prove.

The first proof point is not “AI works.” The first proof point is whether a governed local appliance can reduce friction in a specific regulated workflow without weakening professional control.

01 · Workflow burden

Does the task become easier?

Time to assemble documents, staff touchpoints, duplicate searching, handoffs, rework, and waiting time are measured against the current process.

02 · Retrieval quality

Did it find the right evidence?

Approved-source hit rate, irrelevant retrieval, missing-source flags, stale-document detection, and citation usability are reviewed.

03 · Governance behavior

Did the gates work?

Unsupported drafts, uncited claims, wrong source class, conflicting evidence, and reviewer-unavailable states should block instead of silently pass.

04 · Human review

Did professionals stay in control?

Reviewer edits, accept/reject patterns, escalation rates, and sign-off status show whether the workflow respects judgment.

05 · Audit completeness

Can the work be reconstructed?

The pilot should produce traceable artifacts: source references, retrieval run, draft state, reviewer action, final status, and exportable receipt.

06 · Adoption readiness

Would the team actually use it?

Training burden, staff confidence, points of confusion, implementation friction, and support needs are treated as evidence, not afterthoughts.

The five-stage evidence sequence.

This turns the sales conversation into a controlled operational study. The goal is to know what worked, what failed, what blocked safely, and what should not expand yet.

1

Baseline

Document the current workflow, actors, source systems, handoffs, time burden, and common failure points.

2

Scope

Choose one workflow, one evidence boundary, one reviewer path, and the exact blocked states before deployment.

3

Run

Execute supervised workflows using local retrieval, citation-bound drafting, and human review gates.

4

Review

Compare outputs, edits, blocked drafts, missed evidence, support tickets, and staff experience against baseline.

5

Decide

Expand, refine, pause, or reject based on the evidence record — not demo excitement.

What Pocono AI will not claim prematurely.

Trust improves when the boundaries are visible. This page is designed to prevent the site from sounding more proven than the product currently is.

Not yet earned

Claims to avoid

No fake testimonials. No invented case studies. No universal ROI percentages. No autonomous clinical outcome claims. No implication that a pilot equals medical validation.

Earned through pilot

Claims to build toward

Measured time-to-draft, fewer staff touchpoints, improved evidence traceability, clearer review gates, better audit reconstruction, and workflow-specific readiness.

What the closeout artifact should contain.

At the end of a serious POV, the buyer should not receive vague enthusiasm. They should receive a restrained operational record that says what happened.

Packet Workflow Metrics

These are measured proof-of-value indicators for packet workflow pilots. None are guaranteed clinical, legal, or reimbursement outcomes.

Time & Throughput

  • Time to packet (baseline vs. Sentinel Node)
  • Time per prior authorization support packet
  • Time to appeal-ready packet
  • After-hours documentation friction reduction

Evidence Quality

  • Source citation completeness
  • Missing evidence found (per packet)
  • Stale records identified
  • Unsupported claims caught before reviewer
  • First-submission completeness rate

Human Review

  • Reviewer correction count per packet
  • Human sign-off completion rate
  • Final output used / not used
  • Reason output not used (documented)

Staff Experience

  • Staff friction severity (per session)
  • Bad-output events logged
  • Escalation events vs. resolved in-session
  • Staff confidence change (pre/post)
