Sentinel Node Founding Commercial Leadership
Now Accepting Introductions · Two Roles

Join the founding team.

Help bring secure, locally governed AI infrastructure to independent medical practices and regulated professional environments.

Pocono AI, LLC is seeking two early-stage contributors to help build something the healthcare and legal markets genuinely need: AI infrastructure that remains locally governed, operationally transparent, and human accountable.

We are not building consumer AI. We are not building hype. We are building a fundamentally different kind of AI deployment architecture for organizations that cannot afford black-box systems, unverifiable outputs, or uncontrolled data exposure.

Two opportunities are currently open: a founding commercial leadership role focused on healthcare and regulated-industry relationships, and a technical apprenticeship for someone who wants to learn how real-world AI infrastructure is deployed responsibly inside regulated environments.

Founding Commercial Leadership · First 90 Days

What we expect to happen in the first quarter

No quota. No CRM theatre. A real, sequenced 90 days that begins with listening, ends with the first paid Proof-of-Value introduction, and is co-built with the founder.

  1. 01 · Weeks 1–2

    Understand the architecture, not the pitch.

    Read the executive brief, the architecture page, the pilot evidence framework, and the governance review. Run the live demo. Ask the founder anything. Reach the point where you can explain Sentinel Node accurately to a sceptical compliance officer.

  2. 02 · Weeks 3–6

    Map the regional relationship landscape.

    Identify the 25–40 practices, firms, MSPs, and intermediaries in the regional footprint (Poconos, Lehigh Valley, Northern NJ, surrounding markets) where Sentinel Node has a real fit. Document the people, the practice posture, and the existing relationships. No outreach yet — you are building the map first.

  3. 03 · Weeks 7–10

    Begin trusted introductions.

    Substantive conversations with 8–12 prospects from the map, framed as workflow conversations rather than sales conversations. The goal is to identify the two or three practices ready for a serious 60-day paid Proof-of-Value discussion.

  4. 04 · Weeks 11–13

    Bring the first paid POV across the line.

    Co-author the workflow scope with the founder. Make sure the buyer understands what the POV measures, what it costs, and what the closeout artefacts will be. Sign the paid POV. Become the named relationship owner during deployment.

What is explicitly not in the first 90 days: cold-call sequences, ROI guarantees, fake testimonials, demo theatre. If a candidate would rather lead with those, this is not the right founding role.

How to introduce yourself

A useful email looks like this

No deck. No résumé attachment (unless you want to). A focused note we can actually respond to within 48 hours.

①

What you carried

One paragraph on the kind of work you have actually done — healthcare relationships, infrastructure deployments, regulated-industry sales, MSP operations, clinical leadership. Specifics over titles.

②

Why this thesis

Why governance-first local AI infrastructure resonates with your understanding of how healthcare and legal markets actually work. We want to understand the angle you would bring, not your generic interest.

③

What you want

Be honest about compensation expectations, equity preference, role envelope, remote or regional. We will respond honestly. If we cannot fit, we will say so directly.

Email Marcus directly Replies handled by the founder. 48-hour window in normal circumstances.
Role 01 of 02

Founding Commercial Leadership

We are seeking someone who understands how trust is built inside healthcare environments and who is comfortable developing real, substantive relationships with independent physician practices, medical administrators, healthcare operations teams, clinical leadership, and professional service organizations.

This is not a high-pressure SaaS sales role. There is no quota board, no cold-call script, and no CRM-driven outreach sequence. There is a genuinely differentiated product, a market that has a real problem, and an opportunity to be the person who connects them.

Who We Are Looking For

Strong candidates may come from backgrounds in:

  • Pharmaceutical or medical device sales
  • Healthcare outreach and community engagement
  • Medical practice operations or administration
  • Patient advocacy or clinical liaison work
  • Healthcare consulting or account management
  • Business development within regulated professional industries

What matters more than background is your ability to earn trust slowly and keep it permanently. Healthcare relationships are built over months, not meetings. We are looking for someone who understands that rhythm.

The Opportunity

This is an early-stage leadership opportunity with substantial long-term growth potential. Depending on experience, relationships, and depth of involvement, this role may evolve into:

  • Founding Business Development Leadership
  • Vice President of Strategic Partnerships
  • Regional Commercial Leadership
  • Co-founder-level participation for the right long-term partner

Compensation structure may include meaningful early-stage equity participation, performance-based incentives tied to pilot acquisition, and transition into salaried executive leadership as the company grows and funding milestones are achieved. We can discuss specifics privately.

Why This Matters

Independent medical practices and regulated professional organizations are increasingly pressured to adopt AI technologies while simultaneously facing serious and legitimate concerns around patient privacy, cloud data exposure, governance, auditability, professional liability, and loss of operational control.

Pocono AI was built around a different assumption:

AI infrastructure for regulated industries should remain locally governed, operationally transparent, and human accountable. The person who helps us introduce this architecture to the market will be doing something that matters — not just commercially, but for the patients, clients, and professionals those organizations serve.

Location Poconos · Lehigh Valley · Northern NJ · Regional Healthcare Markets

Candidates located in or familiar with the Poconos, Lehigh Valley, Northern New Jersey, or nearby regional healthcare markets are strongly preferred. We are building in this geography first before expanding. Knowing the landscape matters.

Role 02 of 02

Technical Apprenticeship

Pocono AI is also seeking an early-stage technical apprentice or junior infrastructure contributor interested in learning how real-world AI infrastructure is deployed, configured, governed, and maintained inside regulated environments.

This is not a passive internship. This is a working apprenticeship inside an infrastructure company being built from the ground up — where you will see how decisions get made, how systems get deployed, how governance gets implemented, and how an AI product that takes compliance seriously actually operates.

We are not looking for disposable startup labor. We are looking for thoughtful long-term builders who are interested in learning how real-world AI infrastructure is deployed responsibly inside regulated environments. The people who join at this stage will help define what this company becomes.

This Role Is Ideal For
  • Junior DBAs or database administration students
  • Linux enthusiasts and homelab builders
  • IT graduates or systems administration learners
  • Junior DevOps or infrastructure engineers
  • Cybersecurity-focused learners interested in governance architecture
  • Self-taught infrastructure builders who want real-world experience
  • Anyone genuinely curious about how AI systems are governed operationally
What You Will Learn and Build

Responsibilities may evolve over time and could include:

  • Infrastructure deployment assistance and configuration documentation
  • Vector database and retrieval system support (Qdrant)
  • AI workflow testing and output validation
  • Audit and governance tooling support
  • Linux systems administration and internal tooling
  • Database systems support and integrity verification
  • Technical operations assistance across the deployment lifecycle

You will work directly with the founder. There is no middle layer, no ticket queue, and no bureaucracy. You will see how infrastructure decisions actually get made at early stage.

Compensation and Growth

This will initially be a flexible, part-time arrangement designed around early-stage company growth. Compensation structure may include:

  • Early-stage equity participation
  • Flexible project-based compensation
  • Long-term growth opportunity as the company expands
  • Potential transition into full-time infrastructure leadership as funding milestones are achieved

Mentorship is the real compensation at this stage. If you want to understand how AI infrastructure is actually built, governed, and deployed inside regulated environments, this is the opportunity to see it from the inside while it is being built.

MSP & Regional Infrastructure Partners

Trusted regional operators deploying governance-first AI infrastructure locally.

Pocono AI is actively seeking experienced MSPs and regional infrastructure providers interested in supporting sovereign AI deployments for healthcare, legal, and other regulated industries. We are not building a reseller channel. We are building a federated network of deployment partners who are already trusted inside the environments where this infrastructure needs to operate.

MSPs are uniquely positioned for this work because they already understand what centralized cloud AI vendors do not: local client environments, operational continuity requirements, regulatory expectations, endpoint security posture, infrastructure management cadences, backup and disaster recovery, and the long-term trusted support relationships that regulated industries depend on.

Existing Trusted Relationships

MSPs already operate inside environments where trust matters most. That relationship took years to build. Sovereign AI infrastructure belongs inside it — not alongside it.

Local Operational Presence

Regional operators understand the workflows, staffing realities, and clinical or legal rhythms of their local markets. That context cannot be replicated from a cloud dashboard.

Compliance-Aware Infrastructure

Healthcare and legal environments require disciplined operational stewardship — not installation and handoff. MSPs already provide the ongoing oversight these environments require.

Human-Governed AI Operations

AI infrastructure must remain reviewable, auditable, and operationally accountable. MSP partners are the human layer that makes that accountability real at the regional level.

Long-Term Support Continuity

Successful deployments require ongoing operational partnership — not one-time installation. MSPs provide exactly the support continuity that clinical and legal operations require.

Federated Regional Scaling

A distributed network of capable regional MSPs enables responsible global expansion without centralized control — which is the only kind of scaling that governance-first infrastructure can support.

Federated Deployment Model

Five-stage operational framework

Each deployment follows a disciplined five-stage operational sequence. Regional partners are present at every stage — not introduced at the end.

01

Regional Partnership

Identify and onboard a trusted MSP or regional operator with established presence inside the target market.

02

Local Compliance Alignment

Map the jurisdictional regulatory framework — HIPAA, GDPR, PIPEDA, DPDP, or applicable local law — to the deployment architecture.

03

Sentinel Node Deployment

Deploy the on-premises appliance inside the client environment under full local custody. No cloud egress. No vendor data plane.

04

Human-Governed Operations

Train clinical, legal, or compliance staff on the human-review workflow. Every regulated output requires a named, licensed reviewer.

05

Ongoing Regional Support

MSP partner provides local operational continuity. Pocono AI provides governance reviews, model updates, and escalation support.
