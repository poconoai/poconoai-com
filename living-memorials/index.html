The platform

Living Echo at a Glance

Living Echo preserves more than documents. It helps protect the knowledge, judgment, stories, values, and human context that are often lost when someone retires, becomes incapacitated, leaves, or dies.

The problem

Critical Knowledge Lives in People

Every family, organization, and community eventually faces the same risk: critical knowledge lives inside people. When that person retires, becomes incapacitated, leaves, or dies, much of that knowledge disappears.

Traditional estate planning transfers assets. Traditional document storage preserves files. Traditional memorials preserve memories. Living Echo preserves human intent, context, wisdom, values, stories, and decision frameworks.

The solution

A Governed Continuity Platform

Living Echo captures and preserves human stories, values, lessons, source materials, decision frameworks, and institutional knowledge with consent, provenance, steward controls, and governed access.

Why Sentinel Node Matters
