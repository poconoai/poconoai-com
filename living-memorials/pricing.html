What a Living Echo is — and is not

Clarity here is not a disclaimer. It is the foundation.

A Living Echo is

  • ✓A clearly labeled, consent-based memorial experience
  • ✓Built from a person’s own words, stories, values, humor, and preserved materials
  • ✓Created while the person is alive and able to shape it
  • ✓A way for the people who love you to hear your voice and values after you are gone
  • ✓Private, consent-controlled, and ethically framed
  • ✓A gift built by you, for the people who will miss you

A Living Echo is not

  • ✗Resurrection
  • ✗Consciousness
  • ✗A simulation of a living person
  • ✗A replacement for the person, for grief, for family, for therapy, or for faith
  • ✗A claim that anyone is still present after death
  • ✗Something built or activated without your explicit, ongoing consent

What Living Echo is and is not

Important

Living Echo is not a medical, legal, psychological, or spiritual service.

It does not claim to recreate a person, preserve consciousness, or replace grief, family, faith, therapy, or human relationship.

It is memorial technology built around consent, transparency, dignity, and remembrance.

A Living Echo is not a simulation of a living person and will not be presented as one. Every Living Echo experience includes clear, persistent disclosure that it is a curated echo — built from preserved materials, not a continuation of life.

Living Echo is a founder side project. It is separate from Pocono AI’s regulated medical and legal AI infrastructure work. No part of a Living Echo constitutes clinical advice, legal advice, grief counseling, or emergency assistance.

We are looking for the first early participants.

If you would like to build your own Living Echo, or help a loved one begin theirs, contact Pocono AI to begin the early participant process. There are no forms and no commitments at this stage — just a conversation.
