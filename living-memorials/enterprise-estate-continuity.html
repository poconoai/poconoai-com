The problem

When the Founder or Key Person Is Gone, the Knowledge Often Goes With Them

Operating agreements transfer ownership. Estate plans transfer assets. Neither captures why decisions were made, how relationships were built, what the founder intended for the next chapter, or the operating doctrine that made the business work.

Key-person risk is not just a financial exposure — it is a knowledge exposure. When a founder, partner, or critical leader becomes incapacitated, departs, or dies, the business, the family, and the successors are often left reconstructing intent from fragments.

The solution

The governance layer

Enterprise Estate Continuity runs on the same governance foundation as the rest of the Living Echo platform: documented consent, provenance, steward controls, controlled successor access, and an exportable audit trail.
