What This Raise Funds

The $1,500 friends-and-family goal funds a focused 90-day proof cycle: a working server, local AI processing, secure remote access, and the first handful of real Living Echoes built with real families. It is not a full enterprise build — the founder is also contributing the GPU, technical labor, and launch execution at no cost to the raise.

The preferred structure is a Revenue Participation Loan, not permanent equity: supporters receive a small share of net recurring monthly revenue for a limited time, capped at a modest multiple of what they contributed. No permanent ownership dilution, no forever revenue claim. Full terms are documented in the data room business plan and will be reviewed by counsel before any funds are accepted.
