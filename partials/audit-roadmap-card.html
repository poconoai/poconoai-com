Reviewable Today. Tamper-Evident Next.

Sentinel Node’s pilot architecture preserves a reviewable audit trail for approved-source retrieval, draft generation, human review, and go-live reporting. We do not claim that citations prove correctness, or that a local log is tamper-proof. Our next audit-layer release adds hash-chained audit events, signed verification bundles, admin-action logging, and optional off-box/WORM retention for stronger tamper-evidence.

Citations support human review — they do not prove the AI’s interpretation is correct. Sentinel Node produces cited draft output from approved sources; a qualified person reviews before any clinical, legal, or operational use. “Tamper-evident” here means alteration is detectable, not impossible.
