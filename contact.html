What this is — and what it is not
  • No cloud-first AI pitch. The Sentinel Node runs locally, inside your building, on hardware under your control.
  • No public chatbot workflow. Every output requires human review by a licensed professional before it leaves the practice.
  • No telemetry-dependent model improvement loop. Your patient and client data is never used to train or improve any external model.
  • Local retrieval, local review, local audit trail. Every query, every retrieved document, every human decision is logged in a reviewable local audit record.
  • Built for regulated professional environments. Designed from the ground up for HIPAA-aligned clinical workflows and privilege-preserving legal workflows.
