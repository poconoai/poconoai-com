Day One Deployment Guide

What happens when the Sentinel Node arrives on-site — from hardware setup to document ingestion, staff training, governance, and human review.

Most AI companies explain models. Pocono AI explains deployment. The Sentinel Node is designed to become local, governed infrastructure inside your practice or firm — not another cloud tool your staff has to worry about. This guide walks through what Day One looks like, what happens after the appliance is connected, how documents are ingested, how staff are trained, and how human review stays central to every workflow.

What Happens on Day One

Deployment begins with coordination — not configuration screens. Before the appliance arrives, we work with your team to confirm the operational environment and the human workflow it will support.

  • ✓Confirm deployment location
  • ✓Coordinate with IT or MSP
  • ✓Configure local network isolation
  • ✓Identify authorized users
  • ✓Configure ingestion paths
  • ✓Introduce human review workflow
  • ✓Walk through governance expectations
  • ✓Schedule staff training

Six steps from delivery to first cited answer

1

Hardware Arrives

Appliance ships to your practice or firm.

2

Connected & Isolated

Local network only. No public cloud path.

3

Approved sources indexed inside your environment.

4

Staff query in plain language.

5

Citations Returned

Every answer is source-linked.

6

Human Approval

Licensed professional reviews before action.

Day One outcome. By the end of Day One, your team should understand what the Sentinel Node is, where it lives, what data it can access, what it cannot access, who can use it, and how AI-assisted outputs are reviewed.

Clean Data In. Cited Answers Out.

The Sentinel Node only retrieves from material you have approved for ingestion. Documents are indexed locally, never sent to a public cloud, and every answer is linked back to its source.

Medical ingestion examples

  • Chart packets
  • Prior authorizations
  • Denial letters
  • Lab reports
  • Imaging reports
  • Scanned PDFs
  • Policy documents

Legal ingestion examples

  • Pleadings
  • Contracts
  • Discovery
  • Deposition transcripts
  • Client correspondence
  • Research memos

Ingestion principles

  • ✓Approved sources only
  • ✓Local indexing
  • ✓No public cloud dependency
  • ✓Citations required
  • ✓Human verification required

Local-First Infrastructure

The Sentinel Node is designed around a simple principle: sensitive data should stay where it belongs.

Architecture principles

  • ✓Local-first deployment
  • ✓Role-based access
  • ✓Local indexing
  • ✓Retrieval from approved source material
  • ✓Reviewable, append-only audit trail
  • ✓Human approval before use
  • ✓No public cloud dependency

View Full Architecture Overview →

Governance Is Not an Add-On

In high-liability environments, AI cannot simply generate an answer and disappear. The system must show:

  • ✓What was asked
  • ✓What was retrieved
  • ✓What was generated
  • ✓Who reviewed it
  • ✓What action was taken

Governance checklist

  • ✓Role-based permissions
  • ✓Cited outputs
  • ✓Review tracking
  • ✓Local audit records
  • ✓Escalation workflows
  • ✓Human approval
