Sentinel Node Academic Partnerships — Early Stage Discussions

Building the next generation
of healthcare AI specialists.

Pocono AI is in early conversations with local academic institutions to create real-world pathways into healthcare AI deployment. No signed agreements yet — just genuine interest and open doors.

📌 Transparency note: These are early-stage discussions — no formal agreements or signed MOUs are in place with East Stroudsburg University or Northampton Community College. We're committed to being honest about where we are in the process.

Institutions in Discussion

🎓 East Stroudsburg University

Computer Science · Health Informatics · Business

Early discussions for senior capstone projects, independent study arrangements, and internship placements. Focus areas: machine learning, database management, healthcare compliance, and IT deployment operations.

📚 Northampton Community College

Health Sciences · Business Administration

Exploring internship tracks for healthcare IT and business programs. Potential paid internship roles in customer success, project documentation, and deployment support as pilot sites come online.

Open Roles for Students & Early Career

These roles will open as pilot deployments begin. First hire is funded from the pre-seed angel round. Interns are paid.

QA & Testing
Test Sentinel Node across EHR platforms and practice workflows. Write test cases. Document behavior. Paid internship targeting Q3 2026 start.
Technical Documentation
Write deployment guides, clinical protocols, and user-facing documentation. Flexible hours. Work directly with the founder.
Customer Success
Support pilot practices during onboarding. Learn healthcare operations. Document what works. Paid role tied to pilot site ramp.
Deployment Operations
Hardware setup, network configuration, performance monitoring. Hands-on infrastructure experience with real medical-grade systems.

Neurodiversity-First Hiring

Pocono AI actively recruits individuals with ADHD, autism, dyslexia, and other cognitive differences. Different minds build better systems — especially in healthcare, where pattern recognition and deep focus matter.

We provide public accommodations, flexible scheduling, async-first communication, and a culture that values doing the work over performing neurotypicality. If you're neurodivergent and interested in healthcare AI, we want to hear from you before anyone else.
