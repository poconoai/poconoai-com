5-Year Goal: $1+ Billion Valuation

Built on recurring monthly revenue, setup fees, retention, margins, and repeatable deployment — not hype.

Disclaimer. This page describes Pocono AI’s five-year valuation goal and operating model. It is not a claim of current valuation, not a guarantee of future results, and not investment advice. The goal depends on execution, customer acquisition, retention, margins, and market conditions.

The goal is intended to be driven by hard operating numbers — not narrative.
