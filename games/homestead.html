How to Play

🎮 Joystick to move
✋ Gather/interact
⚔️ Attack wolves
🏠 Near home = menu
🌳 Wood from trees
🪨 Metal from rocks
🍖 Cook for health
🐑 Shear sheep = 🧵
🏚️ Capture dens
🧔 Bram = quests
☀️ Day = safe-ish
🌙 Night = danger
🏰 Progression
Repair 4 parts → level up home (max Lv5)
Clear all 4 dens → outpost safe zones
Cloth upgrades → curtains, bedroll, tent
📚 Library: +1🪵/30s • 🍞 Bakery: +1🍖/60s

⚠️ Hunger drops every 20s (faster at night)
