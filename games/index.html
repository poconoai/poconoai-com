Pocono AI Game Lab

Built for fun.

Welcome to the Pocono AI Game Lab. This is the playground, not the product. Pocono AI builds serious local AI infrastructure for medical and legal workflows. The /games/ directory is where we keep playful experiments, interface tests, cognitive-sustainability ideas, and small prototypes that remind us that sustainable builders still need play.

A quick note: These games are a side project — made for fun, for friends, and for the joy of building something together. They are not a Pocono AI product or service, they don't represent the company's commercial work, and they collect no data, use no accounts, and track nothing. Just play.
