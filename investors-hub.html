Investor Quick Brief — 90 seconds
What Pocono AI is building and why it is defensible.

Pocono AI builds on-premises AI infrastructure for regulated professional environments where cloud AI creates legal, compliance, or data sovereignty problems. The target market — small healthcare practices and law firms — has no credible local AI infrastructure vendor. Sentinel Node is the first purpose-built, governance-first appliance for this segment.
