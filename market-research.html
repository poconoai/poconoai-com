Market Context

Why the Signal Is Real

The demand isn't manufactured. Three structural forces are converging that make on-premises sovereign AI not just preferable but necessary for independent practices.

⚠ Regulatory Pressure

HIPAA Enforcement Is Accelerating

OCR penalties for PHI breaches reached record levels in 2024. Shadow AI — staff using personal AI accounts and consumer cloud tools to handle patient data — is an emerging and rapidly escalating HIPAA governance risk; regulators have begun explicitly referencing AI tool usage in enforcement actions. Independent practices are the least-defended target in the ecosystem.

📈 Market Gap

Enterprise Tools Don't Fit Independent Practices

Epic, Nuance, and Azure OpenAI are built for hospital systems with dedicated IT staff and six-figure implementation budgets. A solo family medicine practice or a two-attorney firm cannot operationalize those tools. The Sentinel Node is the first on-premises AI system architected specifically for practices of 2–10 providers — sovereign by design, not by configuration.
