The clinical documentation burden problem

Independent physicians spend 5.9 hours daily in the EHR — nearly half their working day. Prior authorization alone consumes 13 hours per practice per week. The administrative burden is not abstract: it is time not spent with patients, revenue not collected, and burnout accumulating.

5.9 hrs Daily EHR burden per physician (AMA 2024)
13 hrs Weekly prior auth time per practice (MGMA 2024)
2–10 Provider practice size Sentinel Node is built for

Cloud AI scribes address documentation speed but create new problems: PHI transmitted to external servers, data retention outside your control, no reviewable audit record, and no enforced human review. Sentinel Node is the private AI scribe alternative that addresses the documentation burden without creating the data exposure problem.

What a private AI scribe alternative does differently

Local processing of patient data

Clinical documentation queries are processed on-premises. No PHI transmitted to external AI servers by default.

Physician review enforced

Every AI-generated clinical draft requires named physician sign-off before proceeding. Not optional — by design.

Source-cited clinical drafts

Every output cites the local document sources it drew from. Hallucinations cannot pass the citation gate.

Reviewable audit trail (tamper-evident: Stage 2)

Every query, draft, and review action is logged for 90-day on-device retention.

HIPAA-aware architecture

Designed for HIPAA-sensitive clinical workflows. Zero cloud routing of patient data by default. Your office, your custody.

Governed local infrastructure

More than a scribe: a full AI governance layer for your practice, including retrieval, drafting, audit, and review.

Clinical workflows Sentinel Node supports

  • Clinical documentation AI — Drafts SOAP notes, chart summaries, and clinical correspondence from your local records. Physician reviews before any draft is used.
  • AI prior authorization assistant — Prepares prior authorization packets with supporting evidence from your local patient record. Physician signs off before submission.
  • Referral letter drafting — Generates referral letter drafts from approved local clinical context. Review-gated before sending.
  • Chart review assistant — Retrieves relevant clinical history from local records to support clinical decision context. Not a diagnostic tool — a retrieval and summary tool with review.
  • EHR burden reduction — Reduces the documentation time physicians spend on administrative drafts that require formatting, sourcing, and structuring.

What this is not

Sentinel Node is infrastructure for clinical documentation assistance. It is not:

  • A licensed medical scribe service
  • A clinical decision support system
  • A diagnostic tool
  • A replacement for physician clinical judgment
  • A HIPAA compliance certification product
Important: All AI-generated clinical content requires physician review before use. Sentinel Node does not make clinical determinations, provide medical advice, or certify HIPAA compliance. Each practice remains responsible for its own policies, risk analysis, professional review, and legal and compliance obligations. HIPAA compliance is determined by covered entities and their counsel, not vendors.

Private AI scribe vs. cloud AI scribe

Cloud AI scribe

PHI transmitted to external servers. No control over data retention. No reviewable audit trail. No enforced physician review gate. Data exposure risk in HIPAA-sensitive workflows.

Sentinel Node (private AI scribe alternative)

PHI stays local by default. Zero cloud routing. Reviewable audit trail. Physician review enforced. Evidence-grounded drafts. HIPAA-sensitive architecture.
