Sentinel Node Sentinel Node™ Audit System

How Pocono AI Audits Every AI Workflow

Every prompt. Every retrieved document. Every AI response. Sealed in a reviewable audit log that never leaves your building.

Important: The Sentinel Node™ Audit System provides a structured, append-only record of AI activity to support human review and organizational accountability. It is a governance and traceability tool — not a compliance certification, legal guarantee, or substitute for professional review by qualified counsel or licensed clinicians.

The Core Idea

Verification Is the Architecture — Not an Add-On

Most AI systems treat auditing as an afterthought: a log file tacked on after the fact, stored somewhere in the cloud, accessible to the vendor, and rarely reviewed by the organization using the tool.

The Sentinel Node™ is built the opposite way. The audit proxy sits between the user and the AI model. Nothing passes through without being logged first. Every request is validated, every context chunk recorded, every response captured — and every event is written to the reviewable audit log before the next event begins. The next audit-layer release (Stage 2) cryptographically seals each event.

The result is a workflow where accountability is structural, not optional. Regulators, administrators, and clinicians can trace any AI-assisted output back to its exact source documents, the specific model prompt, and the staff member who reviewed it.

"Verification is not an add-on layer. Verification is the architecture."
This is the operating principle behind every design decision in the Sentinel Node™ system.

End-to-End Workflow

How a Query Moves Through the System

Here is what happens from the moment a staff member submits a question to the moment that answer reaches them — and how each step is captured in the audit record.

1
User submits prompt via Secure Web UI A physician, nurse, or legal professional types a question into the local Pocono AI interface. The session ID, user ID, and timestamp are captured at submission.
2
Audit Proxy intercepts and validates the request The FastAPI audit proxy receives the query before it reaches the AI model. It validates the request, logs the raw prompt, and begins orchestrating the retrieval pipeline.
3
Qdrant retrieves top-K relevant document chunks The local Qdrant vector database identifies the most semantically relevant chunks from your organization's documents. Each chunk's document ID, source file, page reference, section heading, and SHA-256 chunk hash are recorded.
4
Prompt + context + source metadata sent to local model The user's prompt, the retrieved chunks, and the source metadata are assembled and sent to Ollama / Meditron running entirely on-premises. No data leaves your building at this step or any other.
5
Model generates a response The local model produces a response grounded in the retrieved context. The response is returned to the audit proxy — not directly to the user.
6
Full event written to an append-only, reviewable audit log The complete event — prompt, retrieved chunks, source references, response, session ID, user ID, and timestamp — is written to the local SQLite audit database as an append-only, reviewable record. The next audit-layer release (Stage 2) adds SHA-256 hash chaining and signed records so that altering a past entry breaks the chain and is detectable on verification.
7
Human reviews and approves before operational use The response is presented to the user as a draft. A qualified human — a licensed clinician or attorney, depending on context — reviews the output before it enters any workflow or clinical record. AI assists. Humans decide. Always.

Audit Record Detail

What Every Audit Event Records

Each event in the audit log captures a complete picture of the AI interaction, not just the output. This makes it possible to reconstruct exactly what the system was given, what it retrieved, and what it returned — months later.

FieldWhat it captures
User PromptThe exact text submitted by the staff member
AI ResponseThe exact output returned by the local model
Retrieved ChunksThe top-K document segments used as context
Source ReferencesDocument ID, filename, page number, section heading
TimestampDate and time of the event, to the second
Session / User IDWhich staff member or session initiated the query
Prior Hash (chain)*The SHA-256 hash of the previous event — added in the Stage 2 hash-chained layer
Current Hash*SHA-256 hash of this event, sealing it into the chain — Stage 2 hash-chained layer

Audit Integrity — Roadmap

Reviewable Today, Hash-Chained Next

A standard log file can be edited. Records can be deleted. Timestamps can be changed. That's a problem in any regulated environment where the integrity of documentation matters.

Today, in the pilot: the Sentinel Node™ audit log is append-only and reviewable. Every query, retrieval, draft, source reference, and human-review action is recorded locally and can be exported for review. We do not claim that the current local log is tamper-proof, or that citations prove correctness.

Next audit-layer release (Stage 2): the architecture adds SHA-256 hash chaining to make every record self-verifying. When an event is written, the system computes a hash of the current event and includes the hash of the previous event in that record. In that layer:

  • Any alteration to any past record breaks the chain. If someone modifies event #47, the hash of event #48 no longer matches, and the tampering becomes detectable on verification.
  • Records are only appended — never altered. The retention policy enforces append-only writes today; the Stage 2 hash-chained layer makes any in-place change cryptographically detectable.
  • The chain is verifiable (Stage 2). In the next audit-layer release, an administrator can re-compute the hash chain and confirm that every record from deployment to present is intact.

Tamper-evident by design (Stage 2): in the hash-chained layer, each event is sealed at write time, so a written record cannot be altered without breaking the cryptographic chain. “Tamper-evident” means alteration is detectable, not impossible. You can see a working demonstration of this mechanism today in the Evidence Room.

Human-in-the-Loop

Every AI Output Is a Draft Until a Human Approves It

The most important design decision in the Sentinel Node™ system is not technical — it's organizational. No AI output enters a clinical or legal workflow until a qualified human has reviewed it.

The system presents every response as a draft. The review and approval step is not optional and not bypassed for efficiency. This reflects a deliberate principle: AI assists with information retrieval and synthesis. It does not replace professional judgment.

This means:

  • Physicians review AI-assisted documentation before it enters a patient record
  • Attorneys review AI-assisted research before it informs legal strategy
  • Nursing staff confirm AI-surfaced protocol references against authoritative sources
  • Administrative staff verify AI-drafted communications before sending

The audit log records the human review step as part of the event chain, creating a complete accountability trail from query to approved use.

Data Sovereignty

Zero Data Leaves Your Building

The Sentinel Node™ runs entirely on hardware deployed inside your facility. The AI model, the vector database, the audit log, and the web interface are all local. There is no cloud component in the inference pipeline.

This means:

  • Patient data never touches a vendor's servers. Not during queries. Not during training. Not during logging.
  • The audit log itself is local. The cryptographic record of every AI interaction is stored in your facility, on your hardware, under your control.
  • No vendor access to operational data. Pocono AI does not have a back-channel to your audit log, your documents, or your queries.
  • Air-gapped operation is supported. The system is designed to function without an internet connection once deployed.

The audit log is a local asset under your organization's control — not a cloud service, not a vendor-hosted record. You own it. You retain it. You can export it.

Retention Policy

90-Day Reviewable Retention

The Sentinel Node™ enforces a 90-day reviewable retention window. During this period:

  • Records are append-only — nothing is modified or deleted
  • The full audit chain is preserved and verifiable
  • After 90 days, records are subject to automatic purge per the retention policy
  • No records are altered during the retention window — only appended or expired

Your organization may configure extended retention periods or archival export procedures depending on your compliance obligations. The system provides the infrastructure; your policies determine how long records are kept beyond the default window.

Note: Retention periods and archival obligations vary by regulatory framework and jurisdiction. Consult qualified legal counsel to determine appropriate retention policy for your organization.

Honest Limitations

What the Audit System Does Not Do

We want to be direct about what this system provides and what it does not.

  • It does not guarantee HIPAA compliance. The audit log supports compliance infrastructure. HIPAA compliance requires administrative, physical, and technical safeguards across your entire organization. Consult a qualified healthcare attorney or HIPAA compliance officer.
  • It does not guarantee legal compliance in any jurisdiction. What constitutes adequate AI governance in your state or specialty may differ from what this system provides by default.
  • It does not eliminate clinical or legal risk. Human review is required. AI-assisted outputs that bypass professional judgment remain the user's responsibility.
  • It does not provide legal or clinical advice. The system retrieves and synthesizes information from documents you provide. It does not substitute for licensed professional judgment.
