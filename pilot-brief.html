60-Day Paid Proof-of-Value Brief

The zero-integration clinical and legal deployment framework. How a Sentinel Node deploys adjacent to your existing EHR or practice management system — with no FHIR approvals, no IT queue, and no PHI exfiltration — in 60 days.

What the POV Covers

  • Architecture assessment and network isolation verification (Days 1–7)
  • Local RAG configuration against your document corpus (Days 8–21)
  • Document sanitization pipeline setup and index validation (Days 15–28)
  • Human-in-the-Loop workflow calibration with licensed staff (Days 28–45)
  • Live query testing and retrieval quality sign-off (Days 45–55)
  • Day 60 formal POV close: convert, extend, or end engagement

POV Engagement Terms

The 60-Day POV begins with a $5,000 Proof-of-Value fee. This fee applies toward your implementation cost at contract conversion. A full data export is provided at end of POV regardless of conversion decision. Current pricing reflects Phase 1 Founding Deployment partner rates — structured for early pilot partners during the initial infrastructure rollout phase.
